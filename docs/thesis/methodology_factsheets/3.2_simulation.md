# 3.2 Simulation environment

Fact sheet for the methodology chapter, §3.2. Line numbers at commit 337c931 (branch fwd-camera). FINAL-STATE facts only; not chapter prose. M = METHODOLOGY.md. The vocabulary and definitions drafts are being edited by other agents; they are cited "(at 337c931)".

## Facts

- **Software.** Ubuntu 24.04, Gazebo Harmonic (`gz`), ArduPilot SITL (ArduCopter) and ROS 2 Jazzy with the standard `ros_gz_bridge` (CLAUDE.md:18-22). ArduPilot is pinned at commit `7bac20cbcc` (numbers.md:411; tag `thesis-baseline`, CLAUDE.md:933). [NOT IN numbers.md: CLAUDE.md:932 (Gazebo Sim 8.14.0); CLAUDE.md:934 (ros_gz_bridge 1.0.22)]
- **Vendored environment.** The worlds and the floor, wall and ceiling models are tracked under `sim/` and run from there, so a trial's `git_commit` pins the environment (M:831-836; CLAUDE.md:46-53).
- **Mesh provenance.** The iris and runway meshes are not vendored. They come from `~/ardupilot_gazebo` (upstream base `082a0fe` plus three thesis commits), and every trial records that commit in `metadata.yaml` (M:838-846).
- **Vehicle.** The world includes `iris_with_ardupilot`, which includes `iris_with_standoffs`. The downward camera is rigidly mounted, with no gimbal stabilisation (CLAUDE.md:79-90).
- **Time sync is ON.** `sim/models/iris_with_ardupilot/model.sdf:789` and `sim/models/iris_fwd/model.sdf:793` set `<no_time_sync>0`. The vendored model shadows the upstream one (M:3894-3905).
- **Time sync was ruled on correctness:** EKF3 may diverge without it, closed-loop flight needs it, and real hardware is synchronised (M:3884-3891). [NOT IN numbers.md: M:3891 (RTF 0.839 → 0.836, flow latency 22.6 → 21.6 ms)]
- **Dataset flights fly on simulator truth.** `AHRS_EKF_TYPE 10` is set explicitly (`consolidated_rerun.parm:11`; numbers.md:407-408). `LOG_REPLAY 1` and `LOG_DISARMED 1` are set (`consolidated_rerun.parm:13-14`). numbers.md:409-410 records `LOG_REPLAY=0` for the earlier logs and `=1` for future flights.
- **Downward sensors.** The camera is mounted at z = −0.25 m and the LiDAR at z = −0.12 m relative to base_link. The LiDAR range is 0.05–40 m, and it reads 0.075 m at rest in every corridor world (numbers.md:52-55). The LiDAR is a single-beam `gpu_lidar` that raycasts the rendered scene (CLAUDE.md:156; CLAUDE.md:109).
- **Downward camera image.** [NOT IN numbers.md: sim/reference/iris_with_standoffs.model.sdf:239-246 (30 Hz, HFOV 1.047 rad, 640 × 480)]. The flow tracker converts pixels to angle with `FOCAL_LENGTH_PX` [NOT IN numbers.md: src/optical_flow_node/optical_flow_node/flow_tracker.py:16 (554.0)].
- **Optical flow.** Shi-Tomasi corners, Lucas-Kanade tracking and RANSAC in `flow_tracker.py` (CLAUDE.md:23). The RANSAC seed is fixed at 42 (numbers.md:57). Flow processing is fixed at record time (M:777-794).
- **Forward camera (`iris_fwd`).** HFOV 60° (1.047 rad), focal length 554.38 px, 640 × 480 at 30 Hz, mounted level 0.15 m forward of base_link, far clip 200 m (numbers.md:60-66).
- **Sensor rates (sim time).** The IMU bridge realises 333.27 Hz, the ceiling ArduPilot allows at `SCHED_LOOP_RATE` 400 (numbers.md:96-98, 185). Flow and LiDAR run at 30.30 Hz (median gap 33.0 ms), and ground truth at 57–227 Hz (numbers.md:104-108).
- **IMU path.** `HIGHRES_IMU` reaches `/imu/data_raw` on a dedicated SITL serial output (port 14556) that bypasses MAVProxy. The frame is FRD → FLU via (x, −y, −z), and the sensor time is published on `/imu/time_ref` (CLAUDE.md:997-1044; run_trial.sh:300).
- **IMU at rest** reads ‖a‖ 9.81085 m/s² (numbers.md:99).
- **Ground truth** is Gazebo's `dynamic_pose/info` bridged as `PoseArray` (not `TFMessage`). `poses[0]` (the vehicle) is republished as `/ground_truth/pose` with sim-time stamps (CLAUDE.md:131-146).
- **Parameter chain.** Every flight loads `copter.parm`, `gazebo-iris.parm`, `lidar_rangefinder.parm`, `flow_ekf.parm`, `gnss_denied.parm`, `gnss_denied_nomag.parm`, `sim_noise.parm`, `consolidated_rerun.parm` and `roll_rate_gains.parm`, in that order (run_trial.sh:299). `fly_trial.py`'s `EXPECTED_PARAMS` checks the values live (fly_trial.py:55; M:771-772).
- **Rangefinder.** `RNGFND1_TYPE 10` (MAVLink `DISTANCE_SENSOR`), orientation 25 (down), min 0.05 m, max 40 m, `RNGFND1_POS_Z 0.12` (lidar_rangefinder.parm:11-14, 27).
- **Flow input.** `FLOW_TYPE 5` (MAVLink `OPTICAL_FLOW`), `EK3_SRC1_VELXY 5`, `EK3_FLOW_USE 1` (flow_ekf.parm:11-16). `FLOW_POS_Z` is 0.25 (consolidated_rerun.parm:8; numbers.md:213).
- **No GNSS.** `EK3_SRC1_POSXY 0`, `EK3_SRC1_POSZ 2` (rangefinder), `EK3_SRC1_VELZ 0`, `SIM_GPS1_ENABLE 0`, `GPS1_TYPE 0` (gnss_denied.parm:13-43).
- **No yaw source.** `EK3_SRC1_YAW 0`, `COMPASS_ENABLE 1`, `COMPASS_USE`/`USE2`/`USE3` 0, `EK3_GSF_RUN_MASK`/`USE_MASK` 3 (gnss_denied_nomag.parm:45-51). `COMPASS_ENABLE 1` is needed only to pass ArduPilot's compass arming gate (CLAUDE.md:846-860).
- **Sensor noise.** All random sensor noise is zeroed: `SIM_ACC*_RND`, `SIM_GYR*_RND`, `SIM_BARO_RND`, `SIM_SONAR_RND` and `SIM_FLOW_RND` are 0 (sim_noise.parm:29-35). SITL has no random seed to set (M:738-745).
- **Gyro drift is deterministic.** `SIM_DRIFT_SPEED` 0.05 and `SIM_DRIFT_TIME` 5 (sim_noise.parm:25-26; numbers.md:169) give a triangle wave with a 10 min period and a 3.00 deg/min per minute ramp (numbers.md:170-171).
- **The drift over cruise** sweeps the instantaneous bias from ~1.6 to ~14.8 deg/min (numbers.md:172). Its window mean is ≈8.9–9.8 deg/min, depending on the window (numbers.md:173-174; ⚑ see Flags (b)).
- **One drift realisation.** The drift profile is identical in every trial, so it is held fixed rather than sampled. Robustness to other drift realisations is out of scope (M:762-768).
- **Roll-rate gains.** These are a vehicle correction, not an estimator change. The roll rate P, I and D gains were scaled by 1/2.249 so that the roll loop gain matches pitch, which removes a 17.5 Hz roll limit cycle (M:4529-4535, 4560-4575). The values are `ATC_RAT_RLL_P` 0.06003, `_I` 0.06003 and `_D` 0.0016 (roll_rate_gains.parm:8-10) [NOT IN numbers.md: M:4569-4575 (old 0.135 / 0.135 / 0.0036; ratio 2.249)].
- **Corridor worlds.** 20 m wide (x = −10…10 m) and 160 m long (y = 0…160 m). The walls are 5 m tall and 0.2 m thick. The spawn is (0, 0, 0.195), yaw 90° (= ArduPilot North, the flight direction) (numbers.md:46-48, 51; CLAUDE.md:100-104).
- **Floor conditions.** Textured, blank, and transition (textured for y < 80 m, blank beyond) (numbers.md:49; CLAUDE.md:113-116). Floor texture is the only variable between the three (CLAUDE.md:111).
- **Floor construction.** Each world has nine visual-only floor tiles and exactly one collision plane. The blank tiles are the textured material without its albedo map (CLAUDE.md:105-110).
- **World sets** (thesis names): the standard-sensor worlds `corridor_{textured,blank,transition}.sdf` (plain walls, no ceiling, downward sensors only). The textured-wall camera worlds `corridor_*_fwdc.sdf` (line-textured walls, plain ceiling, forward camera, the upper bound). The plain-wall camera worlds `corridor_*_fwdplainc.sdf` (the featureless case) (CLAUDE.md:113-119).
- **Ceiling.** The ceiling slab is 20.2 × 170 × 0.1 m with its bottom face at z = 5.0 m (numbers.md:71-72). It is visual-only and casts no shadow (CLAUDE.md:126).
- **Superseded worlds.** The no-ceiling `_fwd`/`_fwdplain` worlds are SUPERSEDED, because the forward camera sees sky (~46 % of the frame height) (CLAUDE.md:118; numbers.md:73).
- **Room world (Experiments 2 and 3).** `room_tags`: walls enclose x, y ∈ [−5, 5] m, the floor is the corridor checker, and 8 floor tags lie on the lap square (sim/worlds/room_tags.sdf:2-7). The flights fly 5 laps of a 6 m square (numbers.md:520). [NOT IN numbers.md: sim/worlds/room_tags.sdf:2-5 (10 × 10 m room, 3 m walls, 0.5 m tags)]
- **Outdoor–indoor world (Experiment 4).** `outdoor_indoor`: corridor checker floor outside and inside, and a building with an entrance and a plain roof. Walls and roof cast no shadows, so only GNSS changes at the entrance (sim/worlds/outdoor_indoor.sdf:2-8). The indoor segment is 123 s with GNSS off (numbers.md:546).
- **Wall-tag worlds (Experiment 5 and 5S).** `room_wall_tag` (near spawn), `room_wall_tag_far` (spawn 3.9 m from the wall tag, with an occluder panel) and `room_wall_tag_far_occ` (occluder starting in front of the tag) (sim/worlds/room_wall_tag_far.sdf:2-5; sim/worlds/room_wall_tag_far_occ.sdf:2-6; M:10630).
- **Flight profile (corridor datasets).** Arm, climb to 2.0 m, cruise 300 s at 0.5 m/s, land (numbers.md:56). True travel is 151 m per trial (numbers.md:58), and the cruise base_link height is 2.29 m (numbers.md:70).
- **Real-time factor.** The real-time factor is the sim-time to wall-time ratio. The standard-sensor flights ran at RTF 0.975–0.981 (numbers.md:81). The camera datasets ran at 0.72–0.77 (DIAGNOSTIC; measured before corrections; not recomputed) (numbers.md:89, 347-348).
- **Sensor rates are stated in sim time** (header-stamp spacing), not wall-clock (CLAUDE.md:156).
- **Half real-time (RTF 0.5).** The closed-loop corridor flights also ran in `corridor_textured_half_fwdc`, a copy of the textured-wall world with `real_time_factor` 0.5 (sim/worlds/corridor_textured_half_fwdc.sdf:44). Every other world uses 1.0, for example sim/worlds/corridor_textured_fwdc.sdf:44.
- **Standard wording:** "the simulator ran at half real time; the commanded flight speed was unchanged at 0.5 m/s" (numbers.md:464; methodology_definitions_draft.md:206-213, at 337c931). No comparison is claimed across the two real-time factors (numbers.md:464).
- **Why half real-time:** it gives the FGO twice the wall-clock compute per simulated second. The flights show behaviour given sufficient compute, not real-time feasibility (M:7802-7804). All three estimators fly the same half real-time sets with identical timing settings (M:7796-7801).

- **`FLOW_POS_Z`.** flow_ekf.parm:46 still holds 0.13. It is overridden by 0.25 in `consolidated_rerun.parm:8`, which loads later (run_trial.sh:299; numbers.md:213). The logged HAGL is referenced to the body origin (M:1180-1182).
- **Time sync off.** CLAUDE.md:749-755 records `<no_time_sync>1` and SITL's injection of `AHRS_EKF_TYPE 10`. Replaced by time sync ON, with `AHRS_EKF_TYPE 10` set explicitly (M:3882-3908; consolidated_rerun.parm:11; CLAUDE.md:43).
- **Untuned roll gains.** The roll oscillation was first taken as a harmless "background sim vibration" that affected all estimators equally (M:4542-4551; CLAUDE.md Known Issue 1, CLAUDE.md:1681). Replaced: it was a 17.5 Hz roll limit cycle, removed with roll_rate_gains.parm (M:4529-4535; roll_rate_gains.parm:8-10).
- **No-ceiling forward-camera worlds and the `fwd_*` dataset.** SUPERSEDED (sky visible). Replaced by the ceiling worlds `_fwdc`/`_fwdplainc` (M:2328-2360; CLAUDE.md:118).
- **The small textured world `iris_textured.sdf`** is unusable beyond ~10 m: the LiDAR finds no ground past the mat (CLAUDE.md:1686). Replaced by the 160 m corridor worlds (CLAUDE.md:1872).
- **EKF3 no-compass yaw drift "~5 deg/min".** METHODOLOGY.md:879-882 annotates it as superseded. Replaced: +6.6…+11.1, mean ~8.1 deg/min (numbers.md:724-725).

## Flags (b): conflicts

- **Flight duration.** numbers.md:56 gives 300 s for all datasets, and M:4590-4591 a 300 s cruise. CLAUDE.md:1724 costs the batch as a "320 s cruise @ 0.5 m/s" at 386 s per trial (the 58-minute batch, numbers.md:82).

- **Camera-dataset RTF.** CLAUDE.md:122 says "all worlds fly full 300 s trials at RTF ≈ 0.74–0.92". numbers.md:89 gives 0.72–0.77 for the ceiling datasets (DIAGNOSTIC), and numbers.md:81 gives 0.975–0.981 for the standard-sensor flights. Neither range matches CLAUDE.md's.

- **Arming-check parameter name.** CLAUDE.md:835 and 853 call `ARMING_SKIPCHK` "the 4.7 rename of `ARMING_CHECK`". CLAUDE.md:180 identifies the pinned build as `ArduPilot-4.6.0-beta1-6521-g7bac20cbcc`. Version labels only; the parameter files use `ARMING_SKIPCHK` (tags_extnav.parm:11-13).

The time-sync change vendors `sim/models/iris_with_ardupilot/` (CLAUDE.md:43). The sensor SDF that flies (`iris_with_standoffs`) is therefore still un-vendored, and only its mirror is in git.
