## Facts

- **STANDING RULE on metrics:** every ATE states its metric (and window) wherever it is quoted; never compare across
  metrics. Three metrics exist: `evo -a` (SE(3)-aligned), `evo` unaligned, and "apparatus" (cruise window, origin
  gauge-fixed, no yaw alignment) (METHODOLOGY.md:7-19). Amended: the label also names the window; the primary
  cross-estimator metric is evo -a (cruise), computed only by `eval/three_way_eval.py` (METHODOLOGY.md:21-28).
- **Cruise ATE = evo -a (cruise):** RMSE of the position error after `evo_ape -a` (Umeyama SE(3), no scale)
  (numbers.md:197), with the alignment fitted over the cropped cruise span only; pre-arm, climb and landing are excluded
  from both fit and RMSE (METHODOLOGY.md:50-52; eval/three_way_eval.py:20, 103-105).
- **Cruise window (pinned, one per trial, from ground truth, applied to all three estimators):** horizontal GT speed
  smoothed by a centred 0.42 s moving average (numbers.md:194), threshold > 0.30 m/s (numbers.md:193), the largest
  contiguous run (numbers.md:196) (eval/cruise_window.py:10-33 [corrected from :10-37, which was at 337c931; the file is
  now 33 lines]; eval/three_way_eval.py:63-68). On the nine `dsn_*` bags
  it is ~30.0 → ~332.8 s (numbers.md:59).
- **Threshold justification:** pre-cruise speed ≤ 0.001 m/s (numbers.md:199); in-cruise smoothed speed median 0.500,
  1st percentile 0.497 m/s (numbers.md:200); the ramp 0.1 → 0.45 m/s takes ~0.56 s (numbers.md:202). Thresholds of 0.20
  and 0.40 m/s (numbers.md:203) move textured evo -a (cruise) by ≤ 0.009 / 0.009 / 0.008 and 0.009 / 0.010 / 0.008 m
  (EKF3 / UKF / FGO; numbers.md:204-205).
- **Clock sensitivity (bounded, not proven):** shifting the window ±0.25 s and ±0.5 s (numbers.md:132) changes textured
  evo -a (cruise) by at most 0.012 (EKF3), 0.009–0.013 (UKF), 0.011–0.013 m (FGO) (numbers.md:133). The window is
  shared because EKF3's trajectory is on dataflash `TimeUS` and UKF/FGO on bag sim time (METHODOLOGY.md:84-86).
- **Accuracy is scored only on the textured floor.** Transition and blank are NOT ranked by evo: EKF3's output there is
  the invalid origin, not scored (numbers.md:253). Evaluation rule: an estimate the estimator itself flags invalid is not
  scored as a position (METHODOLOGY.md:5321-5323); for EKF3, invalid = `horiz_pos_rel` = `pred_horiz_pos_rel` =
  `horiz_pos_abs` = 0 (METHODOLOGY.md:5323); EKF3's invalid output is exactly the origin (numbers.md:246).
- **Failure metrics on transition and blank:** final displacement magnitude against the true travel of 151 m (the
  displacement ratio; numbers.md:58) and the divergence onset, the true y at which the estimate departs GT by > 15 m
  (numbers.md:206; METHODOLOGY.md:559-561). Window-independent: full vs cruise changes the ratio by ≤ 0.05 and the onset
  by ≤ 1 m (numbers.md:207). Also the position error at +10 / +30 / +60 s after the flow-loss point (numbers.md:248-252).
  These magnitudes carry † (runaway magnitude).
- **† runaway magnitude:** diverging (unaided) FGO results are evidence of divergence with the order of magnitude, not
  precise values; not bit-reproducible across BLAS builds; the UKF's diverging values are treated the same by analogy
  (numbers.md:703-714).
- **Horizontal RMSE in Experiments 2–4 (no alignment):** absolute world frame (Experiments 2, 4) or the first tag's frame
  (Experiment 3), with no alignment (numbers.md:518). Experiment 2: horizontal RMSE over the lap window, mean of 3
  repeats (numbers.md:522); the lap window runs from the first lap marker to `LAPS END`, and `cruise_bounds` is not used
  because corners slow the vehicle (METHODOLOGY.md:10150-10153). Experiment 4: horizontal RMSE over the indoor segment,
  GNSS OFF → ON (numbers.md:548; METHODOLOGY.md:10343-10345). Experiment 3: horizontal RMSE in the first tag's frame
  (numbers.md:569).
- **Hindsight gain (Experiment 2):** per gap, Δ = mean live error − mean hindsight error (FGO live → whole-flight batch;
  UKF live → RTS) (METHODOLOGY.md:10155-10158). Tested by strict increase of per-condition medians plus Spearman ρ over all
  gaps (METHODOLOGY.md:10171-10173); result ρ = +0.30, p = 6e-6, n = 213 gaps (numbers.md:534).
- **Ratio criteria (Experiments 2, 4):** "similar" = the largest RMSE ≤ 1.5 × the smallest (METHODOLOGY.md:10178-10182).
  [NOT IN numbers.md: METHODOLOGY.md:10179 (the 1.5× band)]
- **Experiment 4 return metrics:** error at the GNSS-ON instant and the jump, the largest position step in the 10 s after
  ON (METHODOLOGY.md:10345-10347; numbers.md:550-555).
- **Drift metrics.** (i) Yaw drift in deg/min over cruise (e.g. numbers.md:233, 235) and over the first 150 s of
  closed-loop cruise (numbers.md:476). (ii) Experiment 5 tag-loss drift: end-of-loss |estimate − GT|
  (`drift_end_m`, the metric METHODOLOGY quotes) and the effective drift, last fix → first returning fix (POST HOC,
  labelled, both kept); they differ by ≤ 5 mm on the S2/S3 flights (numbers.md:637-638) and ≤ 4 mm on S1 far
  (numbers.md:626). (iii) The drift bands: EKF3 and FGO 50 / 100 / 200 / 150 mm, UKF 2× (numbers.md:626;
  METHODOLOGY.md:10689-10693).
- **Re-lock time (Experiment 5):** pre-registered as occluder OFF → first fused fix (METHODOLOGY.md:10681-10683); it
  measured Gazebo's occluder render lag (0.16–1.06 s, P-H6 FAIL as worded; numbers.md:617), so the effective re-lock,
  from the first returning fix, is reported POST HOC (numbers.md:616; METHODOLOGY.md:10848-10850).
- **Contact time (closed loop, without heading):** wall contact is |x| ≥ 9.58 m from ground truth (numbers.md:428);
  "cruise +t" is measured from the pinned cruise window's t0 (METHODOLOGY.md:5070). Registered contact windows:
  EKF3 [+155, +215] s (numbers.md:470), UKF [+146, +218] s (numbers.md:471), FGO [+145, +196] s (numbers.md:472).
- **Closed-loop criteria without heading:** EKF3 PRIMARY = no contact before +100 s and contact, if any, on the west
  wall inside its window (METHODOLOGY.md:4946-4948). UKF/FGO: U1 backend take-off (arming, take-off to ≥ 1.8 m, entry
  into cruise, no crash/failsafe/loss of control), U2 no contact before +100 s, U3 west-wall contact inside the window
  (METHODOLOGY.md:7349-7354, 7511-7514, 8994-8997). [NOT IN numbers.md: METHODOLOGY.md:7350 (U1 ≥ 1.8 m)]
- **Closed-loop criteria with heading, per flight, B1–B5:** B1 full mission and landing with no wall contact
  (|x| < 9.58 m, numbers.md:428); B2 max cruise cross-track ≤ 6.0 m; B3 yaw error p95 ≤ 1.0°, max ≤ 2.0°; B4 1 s-smoothed
  |GT lateral speed| ≤ 0.25 m/s; B5 cruise gyro rms roll ≤ 0.05, pitch ≤ 0.0108, yaw ≤ 0.02 rad/s
  (METHODOLOGY.md:4951-4966). A set passes if all three flights pass B1–B5 (METHODOLOGY.md:4979-4983).
  [NOT IN numbers.md: METHODOLOGY.md:4953 (B2 6.0 m)] [NOT IN numbers.md: METHODOLOGY.md:4958 (B3 1.0° / 2.0°)]
  [NOT IN numbers.md: METHODOLOGY.md:4966 (B5 gyro-rms bounds)]. B4's 0.25 m/s is in numbers.md:480.
- **Criterion windows:** B1 the whole flight, arming to disarm; B2–B4 the pinned cruise window, end-wall approach
  included (METHODOLOGY.md:5068-5075). B5 window (D28, current): the longest contiguous run of vy > 0.3 (gaps ≤ 1 s),
  trimmed 5 s at each end (METHODOLOGY.md:9943-9944).
- **Estimator-validity criterion (S2, UKF/FGO closed loop):** the flying estimator valid throughout, plus U1
  (METHODOLOGY.md:7362); for the FGO, `XFGO.OV` 100% from arming to disarm (METHODOLOGY.md:9277). S1: no wall contact
  before cruise +50 s (METHODOLOGY.md:7361). [NOT IN numbers.md: METHODOLOGY.md:7361 (S1 +50 s)]
- **The paired test (H1.1, the one confirmatory test):** paired per-flight d = evo(EKF3) − evo(FGO), evo -a (cruise),
  same flight and window, on 7 new flights r4–r10 only (r1–r3 formed the hypothesis) (METHODOLOGY.md:5644-5648). Claim an
  ordering only if (a) d < 0 on at least 6 of 7 (one-sided sign test) AND (b) the 95% t-interval of mean d excludes 0;
  mirror rule for "FGO lower"; otherwise "no ordering is claimed" and mean d ± CI is reported (METHODOLOGY.md:5649-5658).
  [NOT IN numbers.md: METHODOLOGY.md:5650 (sign-test p 0.0625 at 6/7)]
- **Paired-test result (current):** no ordering claimed; d < 0 on 5/7 (r10 unusable for the FGO, counted against
  both); mean d −0.021 m, 95% CI [−0.989, +0.947]; one-sided sign-test p 0.227 / 0.992; df 5 or 6 does not change it
  (numbers.md:227). n = 7 flights (6 usable for the FGO).
- **Noise floor:** run-to-run SD of evo -a (cruise) on the re-flown textured `dsn`, with a χ² 95% interval
  (METHODOLOGY.md:5664-5667): EKF3 0.31 [0.22, 0.57] (n = 10 flights), FGO 1.30 [0.88, 2.49] (n = 9), d 1.02
  [0.69, 1.96] (n = 9) m (numbers.md:229). CLOSED; it replaces "n = 3 range ± one range-width" as the basis for future
  textured bands; existing bands are not re-scored (numbers.md:736-738).
- **n = 3 caveat:** with three repeats a fourth run lands outside the observed range with probability 2/(n+1) = 50%
  (numbers.md:208); ranges describe the data, they do not bound it (METHODOLOGY.md:131-133).
- **Descriptive results:** reported with n, never worded as an ordering, shown beside the confirmatory result where one
  exists (methodology_definitions_draft.md:80-83). Example: textured `dsn` means FGO 3.44 < UKF 3.59 < EKF3 4.00 m,
  DESCRIPTIVE ONLY, stated only alongside "no ordering claimed" (numbers.md:236). Closed-loop sets on different flights:
  descriptive only, n = 1 per estimator (methodology_definitions_draft.md:90-91).
- **Label definitions (draft, user's choices open):** as worded (methodology_definitions_draft.md:25-28); post hoc
  (:52-56); descriptive (:80-83); confirmatory (:106-110); exploratory (:133-135); withdrawn (:154-158); PENDING
  (:180-187). "No ordering claimed" is the pre-registered null outcome of the confirmatory rule (:110, :116-117).
  Hypothesis-label kinds H (decision rule), C (pre-registered comparison, no rule), X (exploratory), V (validation)
  (thesis_vocabulary.md:134-137). All files at 337c931; being extended by another agent.
- **Withdrawn claims stay in the record**, marked WITHDRAWN with the reason; the replacing verdict is stated
  (numbers.md:33; e.g. numbers.md:226, 561, 696).
- **"Measured before corrections; not recomputed":** exact label for any cited value on numbers.md's not-recomputed list
  (§1 cruise roll/pitch and the forward-camera dataset RTF; §2 P2 latencies; §11 ceiling effect size; §14 timing);
  never described as unaffected (numbers.md:345-358; methodology_definitions_draft.md:217-229).
- **Rounding of displayed values:** the full-precision stored value is rounded ONCE to the printed precision, half away
  from zero, read through its shortest decimal form; applied at table generation by `rd()` (eval/figs_thesis.py:79-89;
  methodology_definitions_draft.md:191-202). numbers.md shows the same single rounding (numbers.md:758-773).
- **Exact cross-check:** each generated thesis display must equal its numbers.md cell character for character, no
  tolerance (methodology_definitions_draft.md:200-201; METHODOLOGY.md:14229-14240).
- **Status labels used for values:** CURRENT, DIAGNOSTIC, ESTIMATE, CURRENT (pre-registered), plus DESCRIPTIVE,
  ABLATION, RECORDED, WITHDRAWN, SENSITIVITY in use (numbers.md:15-19, 236, 474, 491, 701). The tier-1 extension
  files (E2–E6, the ablation) add **NOT RUN** (a run not finished by the lock, counted separately, never a FAIL or
  NOT MET) (`tier1/preregistration.md:168-180`; `numbers_tier1.md:21`).
- **Extension-experiment verdict vocabulary (E2–E6; a separate, narrower scheme from the main thesis's, §0.5).**
  PASS/FAIL judged on the stated count of completed, gate-passed runs; **NOT MET (insufficient data)** only for a
  crashed run or a failed gate; **NOT RUN** for a run the lock cut off; no early decisions — a criterion with a
  missing run is NOT RUN or NOT MET, never PASS or FAIL (`tier1/preregistration.md:168-192`). A null result (a
  pre-registered effect that FAILs) is reported with the same prominence as a PASS (`tier1/preregistration.md:191-192`).
- **Map error (E6; new metric).** Position RMS (plus max, mean) over the 7 non-gauge tags, and yaw RMS/max, of a
  supplied map against the surveyed map (`e6_preregistration.md:166-168`); the P1 input (`numbers_tier1.md:643-653`).
- **Gap retention R (attribution ablation; new metric, EXPLORATORY).** R_i(ρ) = (U_i − F_ρ,i) / (U_i − F_0,i): the
  fraction of the stored FGO–UKF gap a rung ρ keeps, per bag; R = 1 unchanged, R ≤ 0 the FGO is no better than the
  UKF [NOT IN numbers files: `ablation/preregistration.md:217-220`].
- **Effective mislabel rate (E3; new metric).** Among eligible non-gauge central detections, and overall (of every
  central call including the gauge), reported per run and pooled; 0.71%/0.97% at the registered 1% and 3.58%/4.91%
  at 5% (pooled) (`numbers_tier1.md:259-260`).
- **E6's P2 band method extends the main thesis's own n = 3 range-widening convention (above) to a two-sided literal
  band**: [min − R, max + R] of the stored repeat spread, R = range, scored literally rather than as a one-sided
  judgement call (D87) (`e6_preregistration.md:198-210`; §3.9).
- **See §3.9 for the full set of extension-experiment metrics, pre-registration dates, gates and verdicts** (E2–E6,
  the attribution ablation).
