# 3.9 Extension experiments E2–E6 and the attribution ablation

Fact sheet for the methodology chapter, §3.9. Line numbers at commit 4861f3d (branch `fwd-camera`, merge of E6). FINAL-STATE facts only; not chapter prose. M = METHODOLOGY.md. Thesis names (`docs/thesis/tier1/preregistration.md:40-41`): Experiment 2 = repo "T" (known tags), Experiment 3 = repo "L" (loop closure); E5 = the landmark UKF (`e6_preregistration.md:22`). E2–E6 and the ablation are extension experiments: they report on the already-built estimators (no estimator code is changed to produce a primary E2–E6 verdict; E3 arm (b) and the ablation's r6/r7 are estimator VARIANTS, never merged into the thesis FGO) and they do not touch `numbers.md`, the frozen pack, or any existing figure (`tier1/preregistration.md:1-4, 42-43`; `ablation/preregistration.md:40-42`).

### E3: wrong re-identification

- **Purpose (one line).** How sensitive is the non-robust Experiment 3 loop-closure FGO to wrong tag ids, injected at 1% and 5% of detections, and does a Cauchy-robust tag-factor variant do better (`tier1/preregistration.md:277-279, 457-461`)?
- **Recordings.** `T_A_r1`, `T_A_r2`, `T_A_r3` (existing Experiment 3 recordings, all 8 tags). 39 registered replays (arm (a) 18, arm (b) 21) plus one extra timing-only run, all rc 0, none re-run (`tier1/preregistration.md:280`; `numbers_tier1.md:173-174`).
- **Arms.** (a) PRIMARY: the FGO as-is, `--mode lc`, rates 1%/5% × seeds 0–2 (`tier1/preregistration.md:282-283`). (b) ESTIMATOR VARIANT: Cauchy robust weighting (k = 2.385) on every tag landmark factor only, never the gauge prior, IMU, flow or range factor; rate-0 control plus 1%/5% × seeds 0–2, 21 replays (`tier1/preregistration.md:457-471`). Arm (b) is never merged into arm (a) (`e3_results.md:158`).
- **Metrics.** As E2 (horizontal RMSE in tag 0's frame, lap window, per-lap, lap-5/lap-1), plus run-status fields: live divergence onset, first exception time, failure fraction, effective mislabel rate (`tier1/preregistration.md:356-363, 391-412`).
- **Pre-registration.** `tier1/preregistration.md` §2 (`tier1/preregistration.md:275-505`), written 2026-10-21, **signed off 2026-10-22 with amendments** (`tier1/preregistration.md:5-6`). Stage 2e ran 2026-10-22/23 (`e3_results.md:4`).
- **Amendments/clarifications, each dated and before any affected score.**
  - **Estimator-failure rule** (added 2026-10-22 after 5 of 39 runs, before any E3 scoring): a run with keyframe failures or non-finite `.tum` values is **FAILED-ESTIMATOR**, which supersedes §0.5's "keyframe failures do not exclude a run" for E3 only (`tier1/preregistration.md:386-398`).
  - **Terminology clarification of the 2026-10-22 ruling** (user ruling, verbatim, made after 5 of 39 runs, before any scoring): "NOT MET" in the ruling meant "the predicted outcome did not occur" (counts against the criterion → FAIL), not the pre-registration's §0.5 "insufficient data"; a diverged run counts as not satisfying every criterion that needs it, so the criterion is FAIL, never NOT MET (`tier1/preregistration.md:409-424`).
  - **P-E3b.1 clarification (ruling R2 exception)** (user ruling, 2026-10-23, before any E3 scoring; 24 of 39 runs finished, none scored): if the matched arm (a) run diverged but arm (b) did not, that P-E3b.1 run counts as **SATISFIED, flagged**, reported "(a) diverged at t s, (b) OK", no ratio (`tier1/preregistration.md:427-431`).
  - These three are the "dated clarifications in §2.3" the results page cites (`e3_results.md:4-5`).
- **Gates.** G0-L, as E2 (`tier1/preregistration.md:761-770`). Guard verify after E3 run and scoring: IDENTICAL, 13,253 files (`numbers_tier1.md:34`; `e3_results.md:35`).
- **Labels (verdicts, as worded; Rule R2: a diverged run does not satisfy any criterion that needs it).**
  - **H-E3.1 (PRIMARY) at 1%: FAIL, 0/9** — 6 of 9 runs diverged (2 of 3 on every flight), and the 3 non-diverged runs' LC live is far above the stored no-LC live (`numbers_tier1.md:267`; `e3_results.md:81`).
  - H-E3.2, H-E3.3, P-E3.4 at 1%: **FAIL** (H-E3.3 **3/9**, the other two **0/9**) (`numbers_tier1.md:268-270`).
  - P-E3.5a, P-E3.5b at 5%: **FAIL, 0/9**; P-E3.5c at 5%: **PASS, 9/9** (`numbers_tier1.md:271-273`).
  - P-E3.6 (dose–response): **FAIL, 0/3** — every flight has 2 diverged 1% runs, so seed means are not computed (`numbers_tier1.md:274`).
  - **P-E3b.1 (robust, "no worse" than +10%): PASS, 9/9 at 1% and 9/9 at 5%** (rates `numbers_tier1.md:259-260`) — 6 of the 9 runs at 1% are the SATISFIED-flagged case ((a) diverged, (b) did not) (`numbers_tier1.md:372-373`).

- **Map-collapse diagnosis (descriptive, 2026-10-23, after scoring; changes no verdict).** The non-diverged arm (a) runs' landmark map collapses toward a point rather than the true 6 m square, because correct tag factors are relative (vehicle-to-own-landmark) and give no restoring force, while dead reckoning between sightings is soft; one wrong factor therefore pulls two landmarks together rather than acting as an isolated local error, which is the assumption the §2.1 ~+20% arithmetic made and which failed (`e3_results.md:243-259, 358-377`).

### E5: UKF with tag landmark states

- **Purpose (one line).** With tags at unknown positions, does a UKF that augments its state with each tag, re-identifies it and smooths with an RTS pass bound its live drift the way the Experiment 3 FGO does, and how does it compare with that FGO (`tier1/preregistration.md:607-614`)?
- **Recordings.** `T_A_r1–r3`, LC and no-LC, cut at anchor + 245 s — the same recordings and cut as Experiment 3 (`tier1/preregistration.md:616-617`). 6 result replays, all exit 0 (`e5_results.md:17-18`).
- **Arms.** The new landmark UKF (`src/estimators/e6_map.py`'s sibling design, `ukf_lm.py`/`lc_ukf_replay.py`): 15-state vehicle block plus a 3-state [x, y, yaw] landmark per tag, augmented by unscented transform with cross-covariance on first sighting, updated by the same tag-frame measurement model and noise (σ 0.05 m / 5°) as the FGO's tag factor, no gate, no robust weighting, RTS hindsight over the size-changing augmented state (`e5_design.md:53-103, 255-281`). LC (re-identification) and no-LC (new landmark per pass, finished-pass landmarks dropped — the design argues this is equivalent to the FGO's no-LC arm, `e5_design.md:195-254`).
- **Metrics.** As Experiment 3/E2 (horizontal RMSE, tag-0 frame, lap window, per-lap, lap-5/lap-1); live = the state after each predict step; hindsight = RTS over the augmented state (`tier1/preregistration.md:656-660`).
- **Pre-registration.** `tier1/preregistration.md` §4 (`tier1/preregistration.md:607-694`) plus the design document `e5_design.md` (written before the code, `tier1/preregistration.md:608-609`); written 2026-10-21, **signed off 2026-10-22 with amendment 1** (the E5 primary criterion and the secondary descriptive-plus-direction wording) **and amendment 2** (the validation-gate tolerance in place of byte identity for the new landmark code path) (`tier1/preregistration.md:10-14`).

### E6: map reuse ("map once, fly again")

- **Purpose (one line, user's question verbatim).** "Can a tag map from one flight's full-trajectory (hindsight) solve replace surveying the tags for later flights, and does the FGO's map do better than a filter's?" (`e6_preregistration.md:29-30`).

## Flags (b): conflicts

- **"NOT MET" means two different things across the main experiments and the tier-1 extensions, and E3 shows the collision directly.** The main-experiment vocabulary's "TEST RUN, RULE NOT MET" means a pre-registered rule was applied and gave that outcome (thesis_vocabulary.md:530, see 3.6's own flag). The tier-1 `preregistration.md` §0.5 defines **NOT MET (insufficient data)** narrowly, as a crashed run or a failed gate, with **no early decisions** and FAIL used for a criterion whose stated count was not reached (`tier1/preregistration.md:168-180`). The 2026-10-22 E3 ruling initially used the main-experiment sense of "NOT MET" for a genuinely diverged run, then was clarified the same day: that usage meant "the predicted outcome did not occur" and resolves to **FAIL** under §0.5's definitions, not NOT MET (`tier1/preregistration.md:409-415`). Both wordings are on the record; the thesis must use the clarified (FAIL) sense for every E3 criterion count in this sheet.
- **E6's P2–P4 band widths and the pre-registered judgement labels are two different kinds of number in the same table**, and a reader could conflate them: the surveyed bands in P2 ([0.016691, 0.023695] m FGO; [0.033011, 0.040785] m UKF) are **computed** from the stored arm (i) repeat spread, while the "[min − R, max + R]" **method** (one range-width each side) is itself "judgement, not derived from data" (D87) (`e6_preregistration.md:198-210`; `numbers_tier1.md:705-710`).
- **E2's P-E2.5 wording ("does not decrease") and its mechanical reading ("the ordering A ≤ B ≤ C ≤ D holds") are not the same claim**, and the results page is explicit that they diverge at the first step (A ≤ B is False) while the narrower claim B < C < D still holds; a casual reading of "P-E2.5 FAIL" alone would miss that the data support a weaker but real ordering among B, C and D (`numbers_tier1.md:167, 169`; `e2_results.md:66-74`).
- **The ablation's P1 "pass" on `fwdc_tex_r2` is not evidence of robustness**, because the 2.282 m control band on line-textured walls is wide enough that even a sign flip of UKF − FGO at two windows (r4, r5) still reads as "inside the band" — the pre-registration's own "weak test" caveat and this sheet's P1 entry should always be read together, not P1's verdict alone (`ablation/preregistration.md:199-203`; `ablation_numbers.md:144-146`).
