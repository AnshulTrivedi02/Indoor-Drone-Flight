# 3.4 How the estimators were run

Fact sheet for the methodology chapter, §3.4. Line numbers at commit 337c931 (branch fwd-camera). FINAL-STATE facts only; not chapter prose. M = METHODOLOGY.md.

Thesis vocabulary (`docs/thesis/thesis_vocabulary.md`, at 337c931; being extended by another agent): **live** (row 36,
thesis_vocabulary.md:114), **hindsight** (row 37, :115), **smoothed UKF** (row 38, :116), **passive estimator**, always

## Facts

- **Four run modes are used in the thesis.** (1) offline live-style replay, (2) hindsight (whole-flight smoothing after
  the flight), (3) passive estimator ("shadow") in a live SITL flight, (4) closed loop (the estimator flies the vehicle).
  Names: thesis_vocabulary.md:114-117 (at 337c931; being extended by another agent).
- **Dataset flights fly on simulator truth.** Every dataset flight sets `AHRS_EKF_TYPE 10` (the simulator's own state), so
  no estimator under test steers the vehicle (consolidated_rerun.parm:9-11; CLAUDE.md:793-805). Reason: with an
  estimator in the loop "the estimator under test determines the trajectory it is tested on" (CLAUDE.md:795-796); flying
  on truth gives one trajectory per flight, shared by all three estimators.
- **Consequence stated by the record:** no closed-loop stability claim can be made from the open-loop datasets
  (CLAUDE.md:806-807); closed-loop behaviour is tested separately (mode 4).
- **EKF3 in the open-loop datasets** is ArduPilot's own EKF3 running onboard in SITL during the flight, not active
  (passive, truth flying). Its estimate is read afterwards from the dataflash log (`XKF1`, with `SIM` as the reference)
  by `eval/three_way_eval.py` `ekf3_tums()` (eval/three_way_eval.py:73-80). `/ekf/pose` is NOT used: under
  `AHRS_EKF_TYPE 10` it carries simulator truth (CLAUDE.md:757-773).
- **UKF and FGO in the open-loop datasets** are replayed offline from the recorded bag by thin wrappers around the same
  cores (`src/estimators/ukf_replay.py:2-7`; `src/estimators/fgo/fgo_replay.py`). Ground truth is used only for
  evaluation, never as a filter input (src/estimators/ukf_replay.py:7).
- **"Live" (offline replay, F2/T/O usage):** "the current (filtered) estimate at each time" (METHODOLOGY.md:7937). For the
  FGO this is the newest keyframe of the fixed-lag smoother (`self.last`, "causal: newest keyframe only",
  src/estimators/fgo/fgo_core.py:223-226).
- **The replay estimate is causal, but two preprocessing steps use the whole bag:** the OI-2 dejitter is a whole-flight
  linear fit of IMU receipt stamp against sample index, and the IMU-gap rule uses the arming time. The FGO node uses
  causal forms of both (running least-squares fit; gap detection invalidates the output after take-off)
  (src/estimators/fgo/fgo_node_core.py:7-18).
- **IMU dejitter (OI-2) is applied to every UKF/FGO replay** from Phase 2 onwards and is the current `dsn` baseline
  (METHODOLOGY.md:1166-1172). Basis: replays otherwise time the IMU by ROS receipt stamp, which scatters 0–9 ms
  (numbers.md:102) around a 3.0005 ms mean step (numbers.md:101), while EKF3 integrates on true sensor time
  (METHODOLOGY.md:1193-1200). Dejitter-only was chosen over the fitted sensor-time shift because the shift acts as an
  estimator-dependent tuning lever (METHODOLOGY.md:1303-1312). Dejitter validity range on `dsn` ±13 ms, guard limit
  25 ms (numbers.md:115-116).
- **IMU-gap rule (extended form, current):** an irregularity is a gap (sensor-time step > 10 ms, numbers.md:110) or a
  receipt bunch (receipt stamp displaced > 25 ms from the linear map, numbers.md:112). Before arming: the replay starts
  at the first clean sample after the last irregularity, provided ≥ 10 s of stationary data remains (numbers.md:113).
  Otherwise, or after arming: the bag is unusable for UKF/FGO and EKF3 is still scored (METHODOLOGY.md:2487-2500;
  `eval/imu_gap_check.py` → `<trial>/imu_gaps.json`).
- **IMU-gap outcome on the 42 re-flown bags:** 39 `ok`, 3 `trim`, 0 `unusable` (numbers.md:326). One confirmatory
  repeat (`dsn_tex_r10`) is unusable for UKF/FGO by this rule (numbers.md:227).
- **Hindsight (F2, T, O usage):** "the full-trajectory estimate after the flight" (METHODOLOGY.md:7938). For the UKF and
  the live FGO, hindsight = live in F2, since neither revises the past beyond the 0.25 s window (METHODOLOGY.md:7938-7939).
- **Hindsight tracks in Experiments 2 and 4:** the FGO whole-flight batch, one LM (≤ 50 iterations) over every factor
  the live FGO received; and the smoothed UKF, the unscented RTS smoother over the UKF's predict nodes. "FGO hindsight vs
  RTS-UKF is the fair hindsight comparison"; FGO hindsight vs the filters' live tracks is not (METHODOLOGY.md:10144-10148).
- **Experiment 3 redefines live and hindsight:** live = the newest keyframe after each iSAM2 update; hindsight = every
  keyframe after a final LM polish (METHODOLOGY.md:10476-10477). As executed, hindsight is iSAM2's full-graph estimate
  after 5 extra relinearising updates (a recorded deviation; METHODOLOGY.md:10557-10558).
- **Passive estimator ("shadow"), who flies, three forms:** (i) truth flies, estimator passive (e.g. the FGO shadow runs,
  METHODOLOGY.md:8070; the UKF in SITL, METHODOLOGY.md:5679-5682); (ii) EKF3 flies, the UKF/FGO passive (Experiment 5
  S1; numbers.md:610); (iii) a non-active estimator logging alongside the active one (EKF3 in every closed-loop UKF/FGO
  flight: closedloop_ukf.parm:1-3, closedloop_fgo.parm:1-3). Source of the three meanings: glossary_flags.md:93.
- **Shadow UKF in live SITL:** the ported UKF ran in the ArduPilot fork with `AHRS_UKF_ENABLE 1` while the vehicle flew
  on simulator truth, so it "ran in shadow only and could not affect the flight" (METHODOLOGY.md:5673-5682;
  ukf_shadow.parm:3). The FGO shadow equivalent: fgo_shadow.parm:1-3. EKF3 also runs and logs while not active
  (`dsn_tex_r1`: 6654 `XKF1` rows, 2 lanes; numbers.md:424).
- **Closed loop (the estimator flies the vehicle), textured corridor, 0.5 m/s, 300 s cruise** (numbers.md:464). Worlds:
  `corridor_textured_fwdc` (RTF 1.0) and `corridor_textured_half_fwdc`, half real-time (RTF 0.5): the simulator ran at
  half real time; the commanded flight speed was unchanged at 0.5 m/s (numbers.md:464).
- **Closed loop, EKF3:** `AHRS_EKF_TYPE 3` (closedloop.parm:4). With heading: the live forward-camera heading enters EKF3
  through ExternalNav yaw only (`VISO_TYPE 1`, `EK3_SRC1_YAW 6`, `VISO_QUAL_MIN 50`, `VISO_YAW_M_NSE 0.05`)
  (closedloop_heading.parm:1-15). EKF3's ExtNav yaw floor `yawAngErr = MAX(angErr, radians(5))` (numbers.md:402).
  `VISO_DELAY_MS` 25 at RTF 0.5 (numbers.md:464); 38 at RTF 1.0 [NOT IN numbers.md: closedloop_heading.parm:19].
- **Closed loop, UKF:** the thesis UKF ported into an ArduPilot fork (AP_NavUKF, `~/ardupilot_ukf`) as the active AHRS
  backend, `AHRS_EKF_TYPE 12`, `AHRS_UKF_ENABLE 1`; EKF3 keeps running as a passive shadow (closedloop_ukf.parm:1-5).
  With heading: `AHRS_UKF_EXTYAW 1` (closedloop_ukf_heading.parm:3).
- **Closed loop, FGO:** the FGO node (Python `fgo_node.py` for the corridor sets; C++ node for the hold sets) sends its
  state to the fork over SITL SERIAL3 (MAVLink2); `AHRS_EKF_TYPE 13`, `AHRS_FGO_ENABLE 1`, `SERIAL3_PROTOCOL 2`; EKF3 is a
  passive shadow (closedloop_fgo.parm:1-6; numbers.md:466, 646).
- **Builds flown in closed loop** (thesis names: final / ablation / earlier build, corrected-priors node; vocabulary rows
  40–43, thesis_vocabulary.md:118-121, at 337c931; being extended by another agent): UKF earlier build (old UKF,
  jitter) and final UKF port `abb0357d`; FGO original, ablation build (noise fix only, yaw cap ungated) and final FGO
  node `f642cd37…` (numbers.md:466). Every flown FGO corridor and hold result used the OLD priors; only the v4 safety
  re-tests used the corrected-priors node `4c07374b` (numbers.md:464, 490).
- **Fork landing trigger for the UKF/FGO ("thesis-nav position-lost path"):** Copter `thesis_nav_check` runs every loop;
  armed, AHRS type UKF/FGO, `HORIZ_POS_REL` seen since arming and now lost → the EKF failsafe action (`FS_EKF_ACTION` 1 =
  LAND), no origin needed (METHODOLOGY.md:11264-11265). EKF3 flights use ArduPilot's own EKF failsafe instead
  (thesis_vocabulary.md:407, at 337c931; being extended by another agent).
- **n in closed loop:** n = 1 per no-heading cell (flights); closed-loop run-to-run variability is unmeasured
  (numbers.md:464). With-heading sets are 3 flights each (numbers.md:477-480). No cross-RTF comparison is claimed
  (numbers.md:464).
- **Reproducibility of replays:** aided (textured) FGO results are bit-reproducible across builds (agreement to 4e-4 mm);
  diverging (blank/transition) FGO magnitudes are not bit-reproducible across BLAS builds (C++ port differs by up to
  1.97 km on `dsn_blank_r1`) but reproduce on the workstation; they carry † and are cited as evidence of divergence only
  (numbers.md:703-714).

## Flags (a): superseded/corrected

- **`/ekf/pose` as the EKF3 estimate** (Stage B/C ATEs 6.4 / 7.7 / 10.0 mm) is VOID: it was simulator truth under
  `AHRS_EKF_TYPE 10` (numbers.md:781-783). Replaced by EKF3 read from `XKF1` (eval/three_way_eval.py:73-80;
  CLAUDE.md:793-794).
- **Origin of `AHRS_EKF_TYPE 10`:** first attributed to a value stored only in the SITL eeprom (CLAUDE.md:735-738); corrected 2026-09-29:
  SITL injects it as a default when the model sends `no_time_sync` (CLAUDE.md:748-756; numbers.md:407-408). Now set
  explicitly (consolidated_rerun.parm:9-11) because time sync is on and SITL no longer injects it.
- **IMU-gap rule:** the original form (gap > 10 ms only, before-arming trim with no stationary-data minimum;
  METHODOLOGY.md:1985-2005) is superseded by "Ruling 2 — IMU timing rule EXTENDED" (receipt bunch added, ≥ 10 s
  stationary data required; METHODOLOGY.md:2487-2500).
- **UKF/FGO numbers on IMU receipt stamps** are superseded by the dejittered replays (numbers.md:21-24, 785-786;
  METHODOLOGY.md:1166-1172).
- **Live UKF in closed loop, earlier build:** the original (old UKF, jitter) half real-time sets are superseded as
  "final" by the jitter-corrected port `_v2` sets; the full-speed set stays as recorded with the old model, labelled
  (numbers.md:466).
- **Experiment 3 hindsight:** the pre-registered final LM polish (METHODOLOGY.md:10477) was replaced in execution by
  iSAM2's full estimate (METHODOLOGY.md:10557-10558).

## Flags (b): conflicts

- **"shadow" has three meanings** in the record: EKF3 flies (METHODOLOGY.md:7644; docs/tag_hold_design.md:121); truth
  flies (METHODOLOGY.md:8070); a non-active estimator logs (docs/partC_onboard_feasibility.md:28) — glossary_flags.md:93;
  glossary.md:3596-3602. Thesis wording resolves it as "passive estimator" plus who flies (thesis_vocabulary.md:117).
- **"live"/"hindsight" defined differently** for F2/T/O (METHODOLOGY.md:7937-7938) and for Experiment 3
  (METHODOLOGY.md:10476) — glossary_flags.md:94; glossary.md:3482, 3507.
- **`LOG_REPLAY` in recorded datasets:** numbers.md:409 says recorded logs have `LOG_REPLAY=0` and `LOG_DISARMED=0`, and
  CLAUDE.md:1900 says LOG_REPLAY=1 is "not set in any dataset recorded so far"; but consolidated_rerun.parm:12-14 sets
  `LOG_REPLAY 1` and `LOG_DISARMED 1` for the re-flown datasets, and the UKF SITL shadow log confirms `LOG_REPLAY 1`
  (METHODOLOGY.md:5680-5681). numbers.md:409 is a snapshot value predating the re-fly.
- **Closed-loop FGO node language:** corridor sets fly the Python node (METHODOLOGY.md:12582, 12589) while the hold sets
  fly the C++ node (numbers.md:646); thesis_vocabulary.md:118 names a single "final FGO node" `f642cd37` for both. Not a
  value conflict; the node implementation should be stated per set.
