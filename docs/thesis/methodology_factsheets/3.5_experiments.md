## Facts

- **Numbering.** Five experiments: 1 corridor comparison (1A standard-sensor comparison, 1B camera-heading studies, 1C closed-loop corridor flights), 2 known tags, 3 loop closure, 4 outdoor–indoor, 5 tag hold, plus 5S safety tests, which carry criteria and no hypothesis numbers (thesis_vocabulary.md:34-60). The thesis order 2 T, 3 L, 4 O, 5 H is a stated decision. It differs from the order of the repository pre-registrations (T M:10100, O M:10290, L M:10452) (thesis_vocabulary.md:59-60). This is a documented choice, not a source conflict.
- **Label kinds.** "H" is a hypothesis or prediction with a decision rule; "C" is a pre-registered comparison with no rule; "X" is exploratory; "V" is a validation check (thesis_vocabulary.md:134-137). There are 32 H labels plus C1.a, X1.b and V3 (thesis_vocabulary.md:202-203).
- **Common open-loop corridor protocol (1A, 1B datasets).** 0.5 m/s, 2.0 m, 300 s cruise (numbers.md:56), flow RANSAC seed 42 (numbers.md:57), about 151 m of true travel per trial (numbers.md:58), `gnss_denied_nomag.parm` + `sim_noise.parm`, flown on simulator truth (`AHRS_EKF_TYPE 10`). EKF3 is read from its own log (passive, truth flying). The UKF and FGO are replayed offline on the same bags (M:861-865, M:1843-1847, M:5640-5643).
- **Exp 1A purpose.** A three-way EKF3 / UKF / FGO comparison with no yaw source for any estimator: `gnss_denied_nomag.parm`, `EK3_SRC1_YAW=0`, compass unused (M:861-865). The compass-active `ds_*` set is kept only as the EKF3-with-yaw reference (M:875-880).
- **Exp 1A conditions and n.** Three floors (textured / blank / transition; boundary y = 80 m, numbers.md:49) × 3 repeats = the nine `dsn_*` bags r1–r3 (numbers.md:59). Seven further textured flights r4–r10 were added for the confirmatory test; all 10 re-flown textured flights feed the noise floor (M:5640-5646; numbers.md:229, n = 10 EKF3 / 9 FGO because r10 is unusable for the UKF/FGO under the IMU-gap rule, numbers.md:227).
- **Why flights 1-3, 4-10 and 1-9 (methods sentence, decision D122).** Flights 1-3 are the original Experiment 1A textured-floor repeats, on which the EKF3-versus-FGO ordering hypothesis was formed, so the confirmatory test H1.1 was pre-registered on seven new flights, 4-10, to avoid testing a hypothesis on the flights that suggested it (M:5644-5646); flight 10 is unusable for the UKF and FGO under the IMU-gap rule (numbers.md:227), so the descriptive 1A medians and ranges (tables/T9_1A_fitted_vs_raw.md and figure R6) use flights 1-9, the flights all three estimators can be scored on (numbers.md:232). None of the flights is a pilot or tuning flight.
- **H1.1 (confirmatory; standard-sensor ordering EKF3 vs FGO), operative M:5638-5656.** Data: only the 7 new flights r4–r10. Paired d_i = evo(EKF3) − evo(FGO), cruise ATE. "EKF3 lower" is claimed only if (a) d_i < 0 on at least 6 of 7 AND (b) the 95% t-interval (df 6) of mean d excludes 0. "FGO lower" uses the mirror rule; otherwise no ordering is claimed (M:5649-5656). Current verdict: no ordering claimed, d < 0 on 5/7, mean d −0.021 m, 95% CI [−0.989, +0.947] (numbers.md:227).

UKF instability rule: evo > 3× the median (M:5670-5672; 0/9, numbers.md:232).
