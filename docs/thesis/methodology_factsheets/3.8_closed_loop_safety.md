# 3.8 Closed-loop safety framework

Fact sheet for the methodology chapter, §3.8. Line numbers at commit 337c931 (branch fwd-camera). FINAL-STATE facts only; not chapter prose. M = METHODOLOGY.md.

**Scope.** This sheet covers the SITL safety watcher, the room box, the corridor wall check and the failsafe tests (Experiment 5S) in simulation. The real-hardware mocap watcher (G1–G11) and the rung work belong to "Towards real flights", which carries no simulation results (`docs/thesis/thesis_vocabulary.md:55-58`; M:13399-13400). They are listed only under Appendix candidates.

## Facts

- **Contact and crash are judged from simulator truth**, not from any estimator, by `sim/tools/wall_watch.py`. ArduPilot's
  crash check does not detect a wall scrape (`sim/tools/wall_watch.py:2-4`; M:3952).
- **Corridor wall contact: |x| ≥ 9.58 m.** The inner wall faces are at ±9.9 m [NOT IN numbers.md: sim/tools/wall_watch.py:5-6], less the airframe's half-width
  (numbers.md:428; `sim/tools/wall_watch.py:5-6`, :36).
- **Crash rule:** tilt > 60°, or height < 0.5 m after having been above 1.5 m and before the expected landing point
  (M:3954; `sim/tools/wall_watch.py:7-9`). [NOT IN numbers.md: sim/tools/wall_watch.py:7-9; METHODOLOGY.md:3954]
- **A breach stops the flight and is reported, with no retry under changed settings** (M:3955-3956).
- **Stop path.** On a breach the watcher SIGTERMs `fly_trial.py` and `run_trial.sh` by PID, so the bag is flushed and
  `metadata.yaml` written. It escalates to the process group, and then to leftover simulator processes, only if
  needed. Every action is logged to `STOP_LOG.txt` (`sim/tools/wall_watch.py:10-25`).
- **The watcher runs on every closed-loop flight** (launched by `sim/tools/closed_loop_run.sh:117`).
- **Corridor flights with heading:** the watcher also watches the heading node
  (`--watch-process heading_bridge.py`, `sim/tools/closed_loop_run.sh:62`). If the node dies while the vehicle is
  airborne (z > 0.5 m), that is a breach. The wall and crash checks never depend on the node (M:4827-4829).
- **Experiment 5 room box: ±4.4 m in x and y** of simulator truth (world frame); leaving it is a breach. The tilt rule
  stays (`sim/tools/closed_loop_run.sh:74`; `sim/tools/wall_watch.py:44-46`, :120-125). [NOT IN numbers.md: sim/tools/closed_loop_run.sh:74; METHODOLOGY.md:11874]
- **Fail-closed safety watcher (ruling 2026-10-08), every Experiment 5 closed-loop flight.**
  · The watcher writes a heartbeat file every second carrying `box=` and `bridge=` flags (M:10938-10939;
    `sim/tools/wall_watch.py:134-146`).
  · `fly_trial.py` refuses to arm unless a fresh heartbeat shows box=1 and bridge=1 (M:10940; `fly_trial.py:303-337`).
  · It lands if the heartbeat goes stale or a flag drops in flight (M:10941; `fly_trial.py:303-305`).
  · Stale threshold 3 s. [NOT IN numbers.md: fly_trial.py:305; METHODOLOGY.md:10940]
  · `closed_loop_run.sh` sets `WATCH_HEARTBEAT` for all H=1 flights (`sim/tools/closed_loop_run.sh:75`; M:10987).
- **A breach drops the box flag** ("a breach withdraws the confirmation"), so `fly_trial.py` lands
  (`sim/tools/wall_watch.py:142`; M:10943).
- **Watched-process loss** (`--watch-process`, `--dep-loss`).
  · `stop` (default) stops the flight.
  · `land` drops only the bridge flag, so `fly_trial.py` commands LAND.
  · Box and tilt breaches always stop (`sim/tools/wall_watch.py:47-53`).
- **Fail-closed watcher tests, SITL** (`tools/test_watch_failclosed.sh:3-4`).
  · T1, watcher not started: must refuse to arm.
  · T2, watcher SIGKILLed in flight: must land.
  · Round 2 (after a fix): both PASS; detection latency ≤ 4 s (M:10982-10986). [NOT IN numbers.md: METHODOLOGY.md:10986]
- **Estimator-loss behaviour in both port backends (UKF and FGO), "option (i)".**
  · When the external state goes stale, attitude stays valid, propagated from the flight-controller IMU every loop.
  · Only position and velocity time out.
  · The vehicle lands on height plus attitude, as EKF3 does (M:11222-11225).
- **Adapters.** Height and vertical velocity stay valid for `VERT_HOLD_MS` = 15 s after the output was last valid
  (M:11261-11262). [NOT IN numbers.md: METHODOLOGY.md:11262]
- **thesis-nav position-lost path.** Copter `thesis_nav_check` runs every loop. When the vehicle is armed, the AHRS
  type is UKF or FGO, and `HORIZ_POS_REL` was seen since arming and is now lost, it triggers the EKF failsafe action
  (`FS_EKF_ACTION` 1 = LAND), with no origin needed (M:11264-11265; `docs/thesis/thesis_vocabulary.md:407`).
  · EKF3 flights use ArduPilot's own EKF failsafe instead (`docs/thesis/thesis_vocabulary.md:407`).
- **AHRS types in the port tree:** `AHRS_EKF_TYPE` 12 = UKF, 13 = FGO (M:11113).
- **Test hook.** `AHRS_UKF_TFAIL` forces the UKF output invalid. It is 0 everywhere except the UKF forced-invalid test
  (M:11266).
- **Landing behaviour for all estimators:** EKF3's standard LAND failsafe, the 4 s pause included (M:11523-11524).
- **Hover throttle pinned:** `MOT_THST_HOVER 0.310`, `MOT_HOVER_LEARN 1` in `h_extnav.parm`, so every estimator starts
  from identical controller settings (M:11598-11611). [NOT IN numbers.md: METHODOLOGY.md:11600]
- **Flight-controller soft-reboot risk closed for the FGO node.**
  · Pre-arm: if the flight controller has rebooted since the node started, the node is restarted, and arming waits
    for its "clock map settled" marker; otherwise arming is refused (M:11749-11754).
  · In flight: a clock-integrity latch sends every later state INVALID, so the flight controller holds attitude and
    lands (M:11758-11762).
- **No arming until anchored.** Every Experiment 5 world arms only once the backends have anchored to the tag frame,
  with no frame change after arming (`sim/tools/closed_loop_run.sh:79-82`; M:11234).
- **Safety tests (Experiment 5S), pre-registered 2026-10-08 before any run** (M:11251, M:11286-11320). Profile: hold,
  then the failure, then hold (M:11286-11287). Four tests:
  · **forced-invalid test, UKF:** `AHRS_UKF_TFAIL` set in flight; standard watcher (M:11289);
  · **node-kill test, UKF:** SIGKILL of the tag bridge and the flow bridge; the watcher watches the tag bridge with
    `--dep-loss land` (M:11290-11291);
  · **FGO, without the watcher on the node:** SIGKILL of the C++ node; the watcher does not watch it (M:11292);
  · **FGO, with the watcher on the node:** as above, with the watcher also watching the node (M:11293).
- **Hold point for the final safety tests: the 2 m-clearance hold** W(−2.6, 0), 2.00 m from the room box to the north
  [NOT IN numbers.md: M:11873-11874] (the 2 m-clearance hold is named at numbers.md:490). The S-A3 1.5 m drift bound lies inside the 2.0 m clearance (M:11884).
- **S-A1 attitude criterion:** |roll/pitch − simulator truth| ≤ 3° and |yaw − simulator truth| ≤ 5°, from the failure
  to disarm (M:11295). [NOT IN numbers.md: METHODOLOGY.md:11295]
- **S-A2 position-lost declaration:** the "Thesis nav: … position lost" text and LAND within the bound (M:11297-11300).
  · FGO: 0.75 s (numbers.md:490).
  · UKF node-kill test: 5.5 s (`AID_TIMEOUT` 5 s) (numbers.md:490; M:11300).
  · UKF forced-invalid test: timed from when the failure takes effect, the flight controller's "UKF TFAIL" text
    (M:11525-11528).
- **S-A3 safe landing** (M:11301-11306):
  · disarmed within 40 s;
  · maximum tilt ≤ 15°;
  · horizontal drift to touchdown ≤ 1.5 m (numbers.md:490);
  · touchdown vertical speed ≤ 1.0 m/s;
  · no watcher breach.
  [NOT IN numbers.md: METHODOLOGY.md:11302-11305 (40 s, 15°, 1.0 m/s)]
- **S-A4 single landing (node-kill tests only):** S-A1–S-A3 with both landing paths active, and a single landing
  (M:11307-11308).
- **Final runs are the latest run of each test on the final build** (ruling 2026-10-16, item 5): the UKF on the final
  UKF port, the FGO on the corrected-priors node (numbers.md:490; M:14187-14190).
- **Final verdicts** (numbers.md:490):
  · UKF forced-invalid test: ALL PASS;
  · UKF node-kill test: S-A1, S-A3 and S-A4 PASS; **S-A2 FAILS AS WORDED** (5.69 s against 5.5 s; relay drain);
  · both FGO tests: ALL PASS;
  · no breach in any.
- **The FGO safety re-tests used the NEW (corrected) initial priors.** The ORIGINAL flown FGO hold and corridor
  results used the OLD priors (numbers.md:490; M:14187-14190), now relabelled "superseded: original priors"
  [NOT IN numbers.md: refly_priors/numbers_refly.md:376]; a re-fly on the corrected priors is now the current FGO
  corridor (H1.8) and hold row [NOT IN numbers.md: refly_priors/numbers_refly.md:16-23, 97].
- **EKF3 reference (node-kill, EKF3 flying):** landing drift 1.40 m against a prediction of ≤ 0.5 m. The prediction
  was wrong; there was no breach (numbers.md:491; M:11886-11898).
- **Safety tests are flown on short profiles** under the verification-flight rule: ~1–1.5 min airborne, a 20 s hold
  before the failure (M:13104-13112).

## Flags (a): superseded/corrected

- **UKF node-kill S-A2 (as worded vs cause).** Both runs on the final UKF port stay FAILED AS WORDED (numbers.md:490;
  M:13667). The pre-registration fault is that the bound assumed flow stops at the kill, while the SITL MAVProxy relay
  drained for ~0.7 s. A corrected criterion, S-A2b, is pre-registered for future tests only and does not re-score these
  runs (M:13669-13670).
- **UKF forced-invalid S-A2 timing origin.** The pre-registered timing from the PARAM_SET (M:11299; run 1 FAILED as
  worded, M:11480-11483) was RE-WORDED for the re-run to time from when the failure takes effect (M:11525-11528). The
  original run stays FAILED as worded (M:11528).
- **Safety "final" runs.** The earlier final-build runs (the UKF's first re-tests, the FGO on the old-priors node) are
  superseded as "final" by the latest runs on the final builds (ruling 2026-10-16, item 5; numbers.md:490 vs :491).
- **Hold point.** The first safety-test hold point is replaced by the 2 m-clearance hold, after the FGO node-kill
  room-box breach during landing (M:11815; M:11872-11885).
- **Watcher stop path.** The first version SIGTERMed the whole process group; it was rewritten on 2026-09-29 to stop by
  PID first (M:3955 vs `sim/tools/wall_watch.py:10-19`; M:4394-4397).
- **Fail-closed watcher, round 1** (T2 FAIL) is superseded by round 2 (PASS) after the `WatcherLost` fix
  (M:10976-10986).
- **Landing failsafe.** The original landing behaviour is replaced by EKF3's standard LAND failsafe for all estimators
  (M:11523-11524). The re-run used "S-A3 as worded" (M:11524).
