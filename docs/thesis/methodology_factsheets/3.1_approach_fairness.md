# 3.1 Approach and fairness principles

Fact sheet for the methodology chapter, §3.1. Line numbers at commit 337c931 (branch fwd-camera). FINAL-STATE facts only; not chapter prose. M = METHODOLOGY.md. The vocabulary and definitions drafts are being edited by other agents; they are cited "(at 337c931)".

## Facts

- **Same recorded data.** EKF3, the UKF and the FGO are evaluated on the same recorded flight: EKF3 from its dataflash log (`XKF1` in `ardupilot.bin`), the UKF and FGO by replaying the same bag's `/imu/data_raw`, `/optical_flow/measurement` and `/downward_lidar/range`. The gyro the FGO integrates is the gyro EKF3 saw (METHODOLOGY.md:730-736).
- **The dataset flights fly on simulator truth** (`AHRS_EKF_TYPE 10`), so the estimator under test does not choose the trajectory it is tested on. Every condition gets one fixed trajectory, and floor texture is the only variable (CLAUDE.md:793-803). The setting is explicit in `consolidated_rerun.parm:11` because, with time sync on, SITL no longer injects it (METHODOLOGY.md:3906-3908; numbers.md:407-408).
- **Cost of flying on truth:** no closed-loop stability claim can come from the dataset flights (CLAUDE.md:805-806). Closed-loop behaviour is tested separately, with the estimator flying the vehicle (Experiment 1C and 5S; numbers.md:462).
- **Within a flight, every estimator is a passive estimator (truth flying).** The UKF and FGO perturb nothing (CLAUDE.md:799).
- **Same flow stream for every estimator.** The bags record processed flow, not camera frames. RANSAC settings, the RANSAC seed, the focal length and the derotation handling are fixed at the dataset flight and cannot be varied afterwards (METHODOLOGY.md:777-794). The RANSAC seed is 42 (numbers.md:57).
- **Each estimator is correct on its own terms.** Each consumes raw flow, raw gyro and LiDAR and performs its own rotation compensation. No common pre-derotated flow stream is used (CLAUDE.md:951-984). Fairness comes from correct implementation, not identical preprocessing (CLAUDE.md:984-987).
- **EKF3 is set up exactly as ArduPilot intends:** native `OPTICAL_FLOW` input with raw flow, derotated internally with its own AHRS gyro (CLAUDE.md:969-974). The manual derotation in `flow_tracker.py` is a characterisation tool and feeds no estimator (CLAUDE.md:989-997).
- **The same no-yaw condition for all three.** The comparison datasets fly under `gnss_denied_nomag.parm`: `EK3_SRC1_YAW 0`, with the compass present but unused (`COMPASS_USE*` 0) (gnss_denied_nomag.parm:45-49; METHODOLOGY.md:861-865).
- **Why the compass reference dataset is excluded from the three-way comparison:** EKF3 there fuses a compass yaw that the UKF and FGO cannot have, an information asymmetry and not a formulation difference (METHODOLOGY.md:867-874). It is kept as the EKF3-with-heading reference (METHODOLOGY.md:876-878; numbers.md:237).
- **Each trial is labelled positively** by a `yaw_source:` block in `metadata.yaml` (`EK3_SRC1_YAW`, `COMPASS_USE`) read from the flight log (METHODOLOGY.md:885-889).
- **Matched process noise.** The IMU process-noise parameters are EKF3's defaults: `GYRO_P_NSE` 0.015 rad/s, `ACC_P_NSE` 0.35 m/s², `GBIAS_P_NSE` 0.001 rad/s/s, `ABIAS_P_NSE` 0.02 m/s/s/s (numbers.md:142-145).
- **Matching is per second, referenced to EKF3's prediction period** of 0.0125 s (80 Hz; derived, not logged) (numbers.md:184). The UKF port steps at 12.5 ms. The offline UKF steps at the accumulated 15.00 ms with its per-step terms scaled by DT_REF/dt, giving identical per-second densities for EKF3, the UKF and the FGO (numbers.md:186-187; METHODOLOGY.md:9569-9586).
- **FGO conversion rule:** σ_c = NSE·√dt at dt = 0.0125 s (numbers.md:151-152). The four GTSAM covariances are 1.53125×10⁻³, 2.8125×10⁻⁶, 5.0×10⁻⁶ and 1.25×10⁻⁸ (numbers.md:153-156). `biasAccOmegaInt` is zero (numbers.md:28; `src/estimators/fgo/fgo_core.py:35`).
- **Matched measurement noise and gates.** EKF3's live defaults are used: `EK3_FLOW_M_NSE` 0.25 rad/s, flow gate 300 (NIS 9), `EK3_RNG_M_NSE` 0.5 m, range gate 500 (NIS 25), `FLOW_MAX` 2.5 rad/s (numbers.md:146-150).
- **Flow quality is a binary accept/reject gate in every estimator**, as in EKF3. There is no continuous R-scaling, which would be an unmatched extra defence (CLAUDE.md:1141-1147).
- **Matched yaw cap.** EKF3 fuses a zero-innovation yaw pseudo-measurement only when its attitude covariance passes a gate; the UKF gates the same way (numbers.md:158-160). Firing unconditionally is rejected because EKF3 does not do it (METHODOLOGY.md:912-914).
- **Shared-rules principle (ruled):** sensor-validity rules, meaning what a sensor can physically measure, are applied identically to every estimator. Estimator design features are not copied between estimators, because the thesis compares designs (METHODOLOGY.md:7786-7789). Thesis wording: "sensor-validity rules are shared; design features are not" (thesis_vocabulary.md:295, at 337c931).
- **Application: the ground-proximity flow rule.** Below take-off detection and under 0.5 m height above terrain, flow is replaced by "zero translational motion" and still fused. EKF3's own thresholds are used unchanged in the UKF and FGO (METHODOLOGY.md:7038-7062) [NOT IN numbers.md: METHODOLOGY.md:7045-7050 (1 s after arming, 0.1 rad/s, +0.1 m, 0.5 m)].
- **Application: the tag-fix validity rule.** In flight a tag fix is valid only within the characterised camera-to-tag range. One bridge (`tag_bridge_fwd.py`) applies it to EKF3, the UKF and the FGO (METHODOLOGY.md:11613-11624) [NOT IN numbers.md: METHODOLOGY.md:11614 (0.9–2.8 m)].
- **Application: the FGO noise-model correction.** `biasAccOmegaInt` was set to zero as a model correction, not tuning, because the IMU noise must match EKF3's and the UKF's (METHODOLOGY.md:12046-12058).
- **Application: the initial-uncertainty correction.** The FGO's initial yaw σ is 1° and its roll/pitch σ 5°, the same as the UKF's. EKF3's 0.1 rad and 5.73° are recorded as EKF3's design (numbers.md:700; METHODOLOGY.md:12860-12863). The caveat is that the on-ground heading offsets (~3°) exceed 1° at 3σ (numbers.md:700).
- **Corrections, not tuning.** The consolidated re-run allowed no parameter changes beyond the listed corrections, and any result that changed direction was flagged (METHODOLOGY.md:3780-3782). The corrections were applied jointly and are not attributed individually, except the plain-wall trace and the partial attribution for Experiment 4 (numbers.md:693).
- **IMU time: dejitter only.** EKF3 integrates on sensor time inside SITL. The UKF and FGO replay header stamps set at receipt, which scatter 0–9 ms around a mean gap of 3.0005 ms (numbers.md:101-102; METHODOLOGY.md:1195-1199). The replays use a line fitted to the receipt stamps (dejitter) (METHODOLOGY.md:1287-1289).
- **The IMU-versus-flow time shift was NOT adopted.** It was known only to about ±5 ms, and adopting it would amount to choosing a time offset (METHODOLOGY.md:1306-1309; numbers.md:120).
- **Dejitter guard limits** are ±13 ms (validity range on the standard-sensor dataset) and 25 ms (guard) (numbers.md:115-116).
- **The IMU-gap rule is the same for both replays.** A gap is a sensor-time step above 10 ms (numbers.md:110). A gap before arming means the replay starts after it. A gap after arming makes the bag unusable for the UKF and FGO, while EKF3 is still scored (METHODOLOGY.md:1987-1992).
- **Clock alignment between EKF3's log and the bag is bounded, not proven.** SITL `TimeUS` runs 0.016–0.022% slow (numbers.md:121), so comparisons use the offset-and-rate clock map (CLAUDE.md:815-826). Shifting the cruise window by ±0.25 s and ±0.5 s moves cruise ATE by at most 0.012 m for EKF3 on the textured floor (numbers.md:132-133).
- **Documented exception: EKF3 gets no forward-camera arm offline.** It is the fixed no-yaw-source baseline and appears only as the no-heading reference. Only the UKF and FGO receive the camera heading (METHODOLOGY.md:2884-2889, 2947-2948).
- **Why EKF3 gets no camera arm:** one downward flow sensor only; an ExternalNav yaw needs a position in every message and has a `VISO_YAW_M_NSE` floor; and the logs have `LOG_REPLAY=0` (METHODOLOGY.md:2891-2945; numbers.md:402-405, 409).
- **Documented exception: `integrationCovariance`** has no EKF3 counterpart. It is set small (~1e-8·I) and stated as an asymmetry (numbers.md:157; CLAUDE.md:705-707).
- **Documented exception: rectangular IMU integration.** The bridge delivers instantaneous `HIGHRES_IMU` samples, whereas EKF3 receives true delta-angles and delta-velocities. This is an interface limitation of MAVLink and is stated for all three estimators (CLAUDE.md:724-730).
- **Documented exception: EKF3's flow timing.** EKF3 fuses flow 31–44 ms before capture (median 36) because of its hard-coded 100 ms flow interval (numbers.md:231). Its arrival-time pairing on the MAVLink path is a genuine ArduPilot property, and no bridge change is admissible (METHODOLOGY.md:4536-4539).
- **Documented exception: pre-arm gyro-bias learning.** EKF3 learns gyro-z bias before arming. This design feature is not given to the UKF or FGO. It is a caveat on the closed-loop flights without heading only (METHODOLOGY.md:7790-7793) [NOT IN numbers.md: METHODOLOGY.md:7790-7791 (~0.0100 deg/s, ~0.6 deg/min)].
- **Documented difference: per-heading weight.** At equal sensor noise, the FGO weights each camera heading more than the UKF does. This is a structural property of the FGO with untested mechanisms (METHODOLOGY.md:9598-9612). By ruling it is not reported in thesis-facing material (numbers.md:487).

## Flags (b): conflicts

- **Scope of the shared-rules principle.** The recorded wording covers sensor-validity rules only: "sensor-validity rules are shared; design features are not" (PROGRESS.md:47; METHODOLOGY.md:7786; thesis_vocabulary.md:295, at 337c931). Later rulings apply it to sensor noise models ("sensor noise models are sensor properties", METHODOLOGY.md:9569; METHODOLOGY.md:12056) and to initial uncertainties (METHODOLOGY.md:12857-12863). The thesis definition needs the wider wording.
- **Stale EKF3-versus-FGO figure.** CLAUDE.md:1874 gives "textured evo -a (cruise) FGO 4.65 vs EKF3 4.66, indistinguishable at n=3". numbers.md:222-227 gives EKF3 mean 4.00 and FGO mean 3.44 (descriptive), and the confirmatory verdict is "no ordering claimed".
- **Status of the clock alignment.** CLAUDE.md:808-814 says the alignment "must be *proven*" before rewiring. The same file closes it as "bounded, not proven" (CLAUDE.md:815-826). numbers.md:132-133 carries only the sensitivity bound.
