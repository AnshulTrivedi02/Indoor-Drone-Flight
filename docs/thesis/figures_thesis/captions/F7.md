Figure 5.2. Re-identifying tags at unknown positions keeps the FGO's error flat; without it the error grows 2.9 to 4.5 times over five laps.

Experiment 3 (loop closure): the keyframe FGO with AprilTags at UNKNOWN positions (each tag a landmark), flights 1–3, horizontal RMSE per 6 m lap in the first tag's frame, one shared linear axis. Left: with loop closure the live error falls after lap 1 (lap 5 ÷ lap 1 0.16–0.43; overall 0.049–0.076 m live, 0.017–0.027 m hindsight). Right: without re-identification it grows 2.9–4.5× over five laps (overall 0.263–0.361 m live). The thesis's EKF3 and UKF, as configured, have no landmark states, so they have no loop closure; loop closure is not specific to the FGO: in an extension experiment (pre-registered), a UKF variant with each tag in its state bounded its live drift in 3/3 flights, with a higher error than the FGO's in 3/3 flights (UKF ÷ FGO live 1.76–2.08, descriptive). With 4 and with 2 tags, the FGO's loop closure also bounded live drift in 3/3 flights in each (extension experiment). The result assumes correct tag IDs: with wrong IDs in 1% of the eligible detections (extension experiment), the FGO as built lost its loop-closure advantage: its live error with loop closure was below the no-loop-closure error on the same flight in 0/9 runs (6 of 9 runs diverged). One estimator, so one colour, with live solid/filled and hindsight dashed/open. The estimator validation check (V3) is not re-scorable on the corrected FGO (its labels were never recorded).
