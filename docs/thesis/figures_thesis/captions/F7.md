Experiment 3 (loop closure): the keyframe FGO with AprilTags at UNKNOWN positions (each tag a landmark), flights 1–3, horizontal RMSE per 6 m lap in the first tag's frame, one shared linear axis. Left: with loop closure the live error falls after lap 1 (lap 5 ÷ lap 1 0.16–0.43; overall 0.049–0.076 m live, 0.017–0.027 m hindsight). Right: without re-identification it grows 2.9–4.5× over five laps (overall 0.263–0.361 m live). H3.1, H3.2, H3.3 and H3.4 PASS 3/3 [a]. The thesis's EKF3 and UKF, as configured, have no landmark states, so they have no loop closure; loop closure is not specific to the FGO: in an extension experiment (pre-registered), a UKF variant with each tag in its state bounded its live drift (H-E5.1 PASS 3/3), with a higher error than the FGO's (S-E5.5 PASS 3/3; UKF ÷ FGO live 1.76–2.08, descriptive). With 4 and with 2 tags, the FGO's loop closure also bounded live drift (extension experiment, H-E2.1 PASS 3/3 in each). The result assumes correct tag IDs: with wrong IDs in 1% of the eligible detections (extension experiment), the FGO as built lost its loop-closure advantage (H-E3.1 FAIL 0/9; 6 of 9 runs diverged).
