Figure 4.2. True and estimated paths, top-down: with the camera heading the UKF and FGO paths lie on the true path.

Flights chosen by a rule fixed before plotting: (a) of the 9 textured-floor flights of the standard-sensor dataset that all three estimators complete (flights 1–9; flight 10 is unusable for the UKF and FGO), the flight whose mean cruise ATE over EKF3, UKF and FGO is the median (5th of 9); (b) the flight of the textured-wall camera dataset (textured floor) with the same repeat index as (a) if it exists among its flights 1–3, otherwise the median of those three by the mean cruise ATE of the arms drawn solid. Chosen: (a) standard sensors, no heading, standard-sensor dataset, textured floor, flight 3 (cross-estimator mean cruise ATEs, ascending, by flight: 6: 1.85, 2: 2.42, 1: 4.02, 9: 4.46, 3: 4.58, 4: 4.59, 5: 4.62, 7: 4.64, 8: 4.84 m); (b) with the camera heading (offline heading arms), textured-wall camera dataset, textured floor, flight 3 (same repeat index as (a), flight 3). Top-down, cruise window only, truth grey. Each estimated path is drawn after a best-fit rotation about the vertical and a shift (no scale), fitted over the stored cruise window against its own ground-truth file. This gives the same average error as the standard 3D alignment used for the cruise ATE (the RMSE of every plotted path is within 0.005 m of its stored cruise ATE; cross-checks), but cannot roll the path about the corridor axis: the 3D fit is free to do so on a straight path and can turn sideways drift into up-and-down error that is invisible from above. The alignment removes a constant heading offset, so the metres-scale sideways drift of the unaligned estimates (Table 4.1, heading-error decomposition) is not shown; an error along the corridor (for example EKF3's distance-scale error) lies along the path and barely shows top-down. Dashed: no heading source; solid: with the camera heading (offline heading arms, UKF and FGO only; EKF3 has no offline heading arm and is shown as the no-heading baseline). The lateral (East) axis is stretched 3.0× relative to the along-corridor axis, the same in both panels; no log axes. Passive estimators, truth flying (Experiment 1A/1B offline). Cruise ATE (m): (a) EKF3 4.10, UKF 5.17, FGO 4.48 (all no heading); (b) EKF3 4.08 (no heading), UKF 4.96 (no heading) / 0.14 (with heading), FGO 4.54 (no heading) / 0.06 (with heading).
