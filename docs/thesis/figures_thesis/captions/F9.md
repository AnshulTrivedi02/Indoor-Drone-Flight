Mechanism: the controller flies the commanded path in the estimator's frame, so a heading error turns the forward motion by that angle and adds a sideways velocity of the flight speed times its sine; a positive error (estimate clockwise of truth) turns the vehicle west, and integrated over the cruise this is the westward drift into the wall in Figure 4.1.
