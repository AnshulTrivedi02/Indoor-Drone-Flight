Heading error of the flying estimator (its own current yaw minus the simulator's true yaw; EKF3 from its logged state, the UKF and the FGO from their current-time outputs, the values the controller received) against time since cruise start, on the same closed-loop flights as Figure 4.1: textured corridor at half real-time (RTF 0.5; the simulator ran at half real time; the commanded flight speed was unchanged at 0.5 m/s). Colour = the flying estimator. Dashed (here: without heading, not hindsight): without a heading source (1 flight each), drawn to the wall contact (X): EKF3 +242.1 s, UKF +165.9 s, FGO +169.9 s. Solid: with the camera heading (3 flights each). Mechanism: the controller flies the commanded path in the estimator's frame, so a heading error turns the forward motion by that angle and adds a sideways velocity of the flight speed times its sine; a positive error (estimate clockwise of truth) turns the vehicle west, and integrated over the cruise this is the westward drift into the wall in Figure 4.1. With the camera heading all 9 flights stay within a heading error p95 of 0.38° and a maximum of 0.68° (Table 4.3), so on the main axis their lines lie on zero (grey band: the ±2.0° heading-error bound); the inset (linear, y-axis ±1°, the same time axis) shows them alone. Builds: the final UKF port and the final FGO node (original FGO priors), as in Figure 4.1. n = 1 flight per estimator without heading; closed-loop run-to-run variability is unmeasured; no comparison across real-time factors.
