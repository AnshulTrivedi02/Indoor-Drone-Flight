Textured corridor at half real-time (RTF 0.5; the simulator ran at half real time; the commanded flight speed was unchanged at 0.5 m/s), each estimator flying the vehicle (closed loop); true paths from the simulator, top-down, with the lateral (East) axis stretched 3.0×. Walls at x = ±10 m (CLAUDE.md:101, labelled 'wall'); the dotted line is the 9.58 m contact threshold (numbers.md:428). Colour = the flying estimator.
