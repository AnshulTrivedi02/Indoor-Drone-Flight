Experiment 5 (tag hold), one flight per flying estimator: EKF3 in blue, UKF in teal, FGO in orange. The distance between each estimator's own live position estimate and the drone's true position, millimetres, against time; shaded bands mark when the occluder covered the wall tag. The line moves away from its settled level during a shaded band and returns to it once the occluder is removed, though not always in the same direction. Each panel starts shortly before that flight's first steady hold period, leaving out the initial climb and approach to the hold point.
