Extension E6, the same 3 mapping flights and paired test flights as the other two E6 figures. x: the hindsight landmark position RMS of the UKF-built map minus the FGO-built map (one value per mapping flight, both estimators share it). y: the matched test flight's live horizontal RMSE on the UKF map minus the live RMSE on the FGO map, world frame, no alignment, computed separately for the FGO and the UKF (so two points per mapping flight, one per estimator).
