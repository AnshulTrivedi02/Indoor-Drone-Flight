Ablation (EXPLORATORY): FGO window and iteration count against the stored UKF band.

EXPLORATORY: not a thesis hypothesis test; the FGO's own pre-registration for this ablation is signed off separately (docs/thesis/ablation/preregistration.md). Fitted cruise ATE (evo -a, a best-fit SE(3) alignment over the cruise window, not a raw error), 3 line-textured-wall flights (fwdc_tex_r1-r3), the FGO replayed with the camera heading, for 8 rungs of window length and LM iteration cap: r0 = the thesis FGO (0.25 s window, iteration cap 2); r1-r3 = a one-keyframe window (0.01 s) at iteration caps 1, 2 and 100 (cap 100: convergence not confirmed, iterations not logged); r4 = 1 s window, cap 2; r5 = 2 s window, cap 2; r6 = 0.25 s window with robust (Huber 1.345) weighting in place of the heading gate; r7 = one-keyframe window, cap 100, robust weighting. Square markers: Gaussian weighting (r0-r5); diamond markers: robust weighting (r6, r7). Shaded band: the stored UKF with the camera heading on the same 3 flights (0.127–0.137 m). The FGO's fitted error over all plotted rungs is 0.044–0.065 m. Collapsing the window to one keyframe (r3, iteration cap 100) gave a retention R(r3) of 0.97-1.01 against the FGO-UKF gap on the 3 bags, at least 0.5 on 3/3 of them. Against r0 (Gaussian), r6 (robust) stayed within its control band on 3/3 bags; against r3, r7 stayed within its control band on 1/3 bags.
