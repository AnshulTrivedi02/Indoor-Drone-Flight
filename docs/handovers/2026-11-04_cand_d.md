at any point. Scope: Extension E4 (FGO window length vs compute time / live error) and Extension E6 (map reuse), plus

time" and "extension e6".

## Judgement calls, for the coordinator

- **D01 covers two source pages** (walkthrough `ex_E4_err_compute.png`, page 17e, and summary page 21
  `E4_error_vs_compute`) because they draw the identical data the identical way (x = per-flight median step time, y =
  the same flight's live RMSE at that window, one line per flight) - per the brief's "if two source charts show the
  same data, make one candidate and say so."
- **D08 likewise covers two pages** (summary page 19 and walkthrough page 19) for the same reason: the walkthrough
  page is a full-page embed of the summary page's own rendered chart (`fpage()` in
  `eval/figs_results_walkthrough.py`), not a separately-drawn figure.
- **D01, D03 and D04's right panel all derive from the same per-flight live-RMSE-by-window values** but are kept as
  three separate candidates because each encodes them differently (D01: x = compute time; D04(b): x = window
  category, absolute value, two-panel exception; D03: x = window category, change from the 0.25 s value, split into
  4 panels by tag count) and the brief names all three as distinct deliverables ("the compute vs error plot" = D01;
  "the two-panel compute/accuracy trade-off... make it too, as a candidate" = D04; the FGO-window-vs-live-error panel
  = D03). Flagged in `index_d.md`'s Notes section rather than silently duplicated.
- **D02 and D04(a) are the same box-plot content** (median step time per window); D04 is kept as its own candidate
  only because the brief explicitly names the two-panel figure as a separate, user-approved deliverable.
- **E6 panel split (D05/D06/D07):** the brief's source is the three-panel walkthrough chart
  (`ex_E6_map_reuse.png`); the summary page 20 version only has 2 of those 3 panels (first lap, whole flight), so it
  is a strict subset, not a fourth candidate - I used the walkthrough's fuller panel set as the basis for all three.
- **E6 row-id choice per panel:** D05 (first lap, the P4 criterion) points at `P4 (FGO); P4 (UKF)`; D06 (whole
  flight vs the surveyed band, the P2 criterion) points at `P2 (FGO); P2 (UKF)`; D07 (the descriptive map-error-vs-
  live-error scatter, which visualises the P1/P3 sign relationship) points at `P1; P3 (FGO); P3 (UKF)`. Each panel
  points only at the row(s) its own content actually visualises, not every E6 row.
- **D08 is marked EXPLORATORY in the caption body** (first sentence) rather than on the axes, and states explicitly
  that the plotted quantity is a *fitted* cruise ATE (evo -a, best-fit SE(3) alignment), not a raw error, per the
  group brief's specific instruction for this figure.
- **Caption wording for E4/E6/ablation directional results** avoids every D113-banned word (PASS/FAIL/NOT MET/MET/
  MISS/verdict) by stating the measured count and direction instead (e.g. "the live error was lower at the 5 s window
  than at the 0.25 s window on 7/12 flights" rather than quoting the registered verdict word) - the same pattern
  used elsewhere in the already-restyled figure captions (F7's corrected H-E3.1 wording).
- **Legend/label layout fixes found by eye** (not flagged by the automated number-guard, which only checks that every
  drawn number has a passing check, not layout): D02's per-box range labels were originally mis-positioned and
  overlapping (fixed by computing each label's position from the box's own top, not a hand-guessed constant, then
  raised again when the fix for item 5 below made the label font bigger); D03's 5-category x-tick labels overlapped
  in the narrow 4-panel layout (fixed with a 90-degree rotation); D07's y-axis label was clipped off the top of a
  3.8 in-tall figure (the un-wrapped string was longer, rotated 90 degrees, than the figure was tall - fixed by
  wrapping it to two lines and shortening the x label to match); D08's legend originally sat on top of the shaded
  UKF band and its dashed bounds (fixed by moving it to the empty gap between the FGO points and the band, and
  shortening the three legend labels so the box is small enough to fit there). D04 has no per-box value labels at
  all (only the budget-line label) - the first version of this handover wrongly said D04(a) had the same mis-placed
  labels as D02; it does not, and nothing in D04 needed a label-position fix.

## Fixes made after the advisor review

An `advisor()` call before the first intended commit found several defects my own generator's checks could not see
(they check that a number is EXACT and that no banned word appears, not that the sentence around the number is
correct or that the figure matches what it claims). All were fixed, the generator re-run (98/98), every changed PNG
re-viewed, and the banned-word/pointer regex re-run over all 8 captions by hand:

1. **D01's caption printed a literal `(True)`** - `{VMX}` interpolated the raw boolean check string into a sentence.
   Dropped the parenthetical; the fact ("no window moved... by 0.01 m or more") is now stated plainly.
2. **D08's caption said "Circle markers: Gaussian weighting"** but the plotted marker is `MK["FGO"]` = a square.
   Fixed to "Square markers".
3. **Two restated-verdict phrasings** (the D113 failure mode the restyle itself hit on F2/F9, invisible to a
   banned-word regex): D08's "this is the P2 (with heading) criterion, satisfied on..." reworded to state the
   measured R(r3) value and the raw count against the registered threshold, without the word "satisfied"; D03's "(a
   direction fixed before the flights, at least 10 of 12 needed)" and D08's "(6 of 6 needed at every rung)" dropped -
   both stated a count next to its own pass threshold, which states the verdict in different words. The counts stay;
   the "needed" framing does not.
4. **"Flying" is wrong for an offline replay.** E4 and E6 are both replays of already-recorded flights (not
   closed-loop); "colour = the estimator flying" (D05), "flying on the surveyed..." (D06) and "FGO flying with the
   camera heading" (D08) all said the estimator flew the vehicle. Reworded to "the estimator" (D05), "estimated
   using the map" (D06), "the FGO replayed with the camera heading" (D08).
5. **Fonts below the 9 pt floor** (RS-1; the same floor D120 raised F2/F9 to for the same reason): D02's box-value
   labels (8.5 pt), D03's x-tick labels (8.5 pt), D08's x-tick labels (8 pt) and its legend (8.5 pt) were all raised
   to >= 9 pt, then re-viewed and nudged for the new collisions this caused (D02's labels needed more vertical
   clearance from their boxes once the font grew).
6. **The number guard only scanned text drawn on the figure, not the caption.** `save_fig()` now also runs the
   NUMTOK scan over `caption_body` against the same per-figure allowed set, and separately scans the caption for the
   D113 banned-word list (previously only checked by hand after the fact). This caught about a dozen real gaps:
   "97% and 101%" (a derived restatement of the already-stated 0.97-1.01 range - dropped, not re-checked), "5 laps",
   "8, 4, 2, 1 tags, 3 repeats each", "0.01 m" (the H-E4.3 bound), "lap-1", a flight id ("r1b"), "8 rungs" and the
   "0.5" P2 pass threshold on R(r3) - each either got its own check or was removed from the prose.
7. **Hand-typed expected strings had no file provenance** - `check()` previously only compared my computed value
   against a constant I typed myself (so e.g. `"33" == "33"` always trivially passes, proving nothing about the
   thesis source). Added a `shown=` parameter (same role and name as `figs_walkthrough_extra.py`'s own): every
   EXP4/_E4R25 range, the E6 lap-1/ratio ranges, the ablation's stored UKF range and R(r3) range, and the "33 ms
   budget"/"0.25 < 0.5 < 1 < 2 < 5 s" phrasing now additionally assert the substring is verbatim present in
   `tier1/e4_results.md`, `tier1/e6_results.md`, `ablation/ablation_numbers.md` or `tier1/preregistration.md`
   respectively (file/line references given in the handover's checks list below). D06's per-estimator bands and
   D03's VBAB/VBC/VBD change bounds are the one remaining exception: they are not quoted anywhere in prose form in
   the source `.md` files (the upstream `figs_thesis_summary.py` doesn't `shown=`-verify them either), so they stay
   as plain numeric checks against the stored JSON, matching the rigor level of their own upstream originals rather
   than inventing a stricter standard than the scripts they were copied from.
8. **D08's pointer named `P1 (control, no heading)`, but P1 is not what the figure shows.** The chart plots the
   WITH-HEADING FGO arm of every rung (`ABR["runs"][f"{g}.{b}.heading"]`); P1 is scored on the NO-HEADING control
   arm, a different, unplotted dataset. Removed the P1 sentence and its `n_within` check entirely, replaced the
   pointer with `P2 (with heading); P3 (descriptive)`, and added new counts for P3 (how many of the 3 plotted
   line-textured bags r6/r7 stay within their own control band), recomputed from P3's own stored per-bag flags
   filtered to only the 3 bags this figure draws - not the headline 4/6 and 2/6 counts, which also cover 3 unplotted
   plain-wall bags.
9. **D06's band description ("widened by its own range") was my own paraphrase**, not a quote. Looked up the actual
   derivation in `tier1/e6_preregistration.md:208` and now quotes it verbatim ("the band extends the observed repeat
   interval by one range on each side"), with a `shown=` check tying that exact sentence to that file and line.
   Also fixed the band's fill colour from `GRID` (#e4e4e4, the gridline colour - could read as background shading)
   to `TRUTH` (#999999, the house "truth/reference/limit" grey) at alpha 0.35, matching the style rule that a grey
   band denotes a pass limit or reference range, not a gridline.
10. **Two smaller wording fixes.** D07's "on FGO 3/3 UKF 3/3" (a raw check-string dump) reworded to "on 3 of 3
    mapping flights for each estimator". D04's "(a user-approved exception to the one-shared-scale rule, because...)"
    was a process/compliance note about the figure, not a description of what the figure shows - D113's own rule is
    that a caption describes data, not the house rules that were followed; dropped the explanatory clause, kept
    "Two panels on different y-scales" as a plain factual statement, and moved the exception's justification to
    `index_d.md`'s own Notes section where the earlier draft already had a related note.
11. **Sibling-candidate cross-references inside captions** ("the same 12 flights as D01", "the same 3 test flights as
    D05") were replaced with self-contained descriptions. This was not on the advisor's list but was forced by fix 6
    above: `NUMTOK` matches the bare digit in "D01"/"D05" as a stray number, and more importantly a caption should
    not depend on knowing another candidate's assigned id, which the user may renumber or drop when choosing figures
    for the thesis.
12. **Confirmed every PDF is still 453.6 pt (160 mm) wide** after all of the above (`pdfinfo docs/thesis/
    figures_thesis/candidates/D0*.pdf`, checked individually) - unchanged by the font/layout fixes.
