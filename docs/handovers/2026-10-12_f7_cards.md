## 1. PRE-REGISTRATION of the SITL bench `wbench3_f7` (written and committed BEFORE any run)

### 1.1 What is under test

**The F7 rule (`realflight/watcher/yawcheck.py`, wired into `core.py`, `fc_link.py`, `mocap_watch.py`):**
- **While the FC is DISARMED,** the watcher withholds its confirmation unless all of these hold:
  - the FC's own yaw (MAVLink `ATTITUDE.yaw`, the AHRS yaw) agrees with the mocap rigid-body heading plus the tie;
  - the agreement is within **5°**, held **continuously for ≥ 1 s**;
  - the mocap pose is valid (fresh and tracked), and the FC attitude is fresh (≤ 0.5 s).
- Withholding the confirmation means:
  - no MAVLink HEARTBEAT, so the FC cannot arm. The native GCS failsafe refuses once the watcher was seen and lost;
    the Lua aux-auth refuses when it was never seen;
  - the file flag `box=0`, so the flight script refuses.
- Each FC sample is compared with the best-matching mocap sample of the preceding 0.3 s (the FC-telemetry latency
  allowance, as in the identity check). A single comparison outside 5°, a stale FC attitude or an invalid mocap pose
  restarts the 1 s. The check runs on every ATTITUDE (25 Hz) for as long as the FC is disarmed.
- **Once the FC reports ARMED it is INFORMATIONAL ONLY.**
  - The check still runs and is logged ("INFORMATIONAL", `mocap_watch_yaw.csv`).
  - It never withdraws the confirmation, never latches an episode and never sends a LAND.
  - After the disarm it gates again.
- **A withdrawal on yaw while disarmed is not a breach:** no latch and no LAND.
- **Frames.** Both yaws are NED-sense headings (clockwise from above), wrapped to (−180°, 180°].
  - The mocap heading is `euler_frd(R)[2]`: the rigid body's +x (nose) axis, measured clockwise from the mocap +x
    axis.
  - The **tie** is the FC yaw of a vehicle whose nose points along the mocap +x axis: the bearing of the mocap +x axis
    in the FC's yaw frame.
  - Expected FC yaw = mocap heading + tie, and the difference is wrapped.
- **How the tie is set (`--yaw-tie`):**
  - **`origin`** (the default; required for the no-compass variant). The watcher CAPTURES the tie at the FC's
    EKF-origin event.
    - The AHRS broadcasts `GPS_GLOBAL_ORIGIN` on every channel ONCE, when the origin first becomes valid
      (`AP_AHRS.cpp:428-440`).
    - At the first one in an FC boot, the tie is the circular mean of wrap(FC yaw − mocap heading) over 1 s. The
      capture needs ≥ 10 comparisons, mocap valid throughout, and the vehicle still (spread ≤ 2°); otherwise it fails
      and the watcher refuses until the FC reboots.
    - Later broadcasts in the same boot are ignored (Copter re-sends it when home is set, `AP_AHRS.cpp:3072`).
    - An FC reboot (`ATTITUDE.time_boot_ms` going backwards) drops the tie.
    - With no compass EKF3 starts at yaw 0 (`AP_NavEKF3_core.cpp:527`), so its frame is fixed only by the boot
      heading. That is why the tie is taken at the origin step and not surveyed.
  - **a number**: a surveyed constant, the bearing of the mocap +x axis (compass variant only).
- **Residuals (stated):**
  - The watcher must be running BEFORE the origin is set. If it starts later, or is restarted within the boot, it never
    ties and refuses until the FC reboots (fail-closed).
  - An FC yaw already wrong at the origin event is baked into the tie. With no compass there is no absolute yaw to be
    wrong against; the check then guards the yaw CHANGES from the origin step to arming.
- **Offline result before this pre-registration:** `pytest realflight/` → **106 passed** (the 91 before plus 15 new
  in `realflight/watcher/tests/test_yaw.py`):
  - pass after 1 s of agreement, and not before;
  - refuse at 6°, pass at 4°;
  - the wrap at ±180° (both directions, and with a tie);
  - the SITL tie of 90°;
  - mocap untracked and mocap stopped → refuse;
  - a stale FC attitude → refuse;
  - a disarmed withdrawal: no LAND, no latch;
  - **armed: a 20° disagreement keeps the confirmation, with no LAND and no ALERT**;
  - the origin capture: before / after / rotation / a later origin ignored / wrap / capture fails if moving or if the
    mocap is invalid / a reboot drops the tie;
  - end to end with the FakeFC: refuse → heartbeat withdrawn → the FC model refuses to arm → allowed again; armed →
    heartbeat continues, no LAND.

### 1.2 Set-up

- **Session `f7`:** `tools/run_wbench.sh f7 --prefix wbench3` writes `~/thesis_data/wbench/wbench3_f7_<stamp>`.
  - One simulation at a time, under FLIGHT_LOCK.
  - The stack is the 2026-10-12 bench stack: `iris_textured`, camera flow, Gazebo LiDAR, the relay, a simulated mocap
    from Gazebo truth, and the ground parameter file (`DISARM_DELAY 0`).
  - Plus `realflight/sitl_bench/params/wbench_f7.parm`:
    - the rung-1 **no-compass** variant (`EK3_SRC1_YAW 0`, `COMPASS_USE* 0`);
    - **`SCR_ENABLE 1`**, with `watcher_arming_auth.lua` in the SITL `scripts/` before the FIRST boot.
  - Why no compass: with the compass, A9 measured the compass self-correcting an unseen rotation at ~1.4°/s. A 15°
    error would then clear itself in ~10 s, and the rotate-back would create an opposite error. "Rotate back →
    allowed" would then depend on timing. No compass is also rung 1's recommended variant.
- **Watcher:** `--yaw-check require --yaw-tie origin --lua-auth`, identity off, as in every SITL bench session. It is
  started BEFORE the origin is sent (`bench.setup_f7`), as the flight cards now require, and it is NEVER restarted in
  the session (a restart would lose the tie).
  - The `flow_tracker` stub (a watched dependency) is held down from the watcher start until F7-0's rotation, so the
    watcher cannot confirm before F7-0. Without it, the yaw check would be the only check left.
- **Origin:** exactly as R1 of the 2026-10-12 re-run (`establish_origin`):
  - gated on every EKF3 core reporting initialised;
  - verified by `GPS_GLOBAL_ORIGIN`;
  - and the dataflash `ORGN` record, checked after the session.
- **Stimulus:** "rotate unseen by the gyros", as F7 happened. This is a Gazebo `set_pose` of the disarmed vehicle to
  its current x/y, level, with its ENU yaw changed by **+15°** from the boot heading (3× the tolerance; F7 was 22°).
  "Rotate back" is the same call with the boot heading.
- **Arm probes:**
  - (i) the bench operator's **non-force** MAVLink arm in GUIDED (system ID 255, as MAVProxy);
  - (ii) the flight script (`fly_trial.py`'s own `require_watcher_before_arming`, imported read-only), which waits up
    to 60 s for a confirmation.
