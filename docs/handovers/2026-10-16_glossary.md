# Handover 2026-10-16: project-wide glossary (one stage, read-only)

This stage was run by a stage agent on a worktree branch based on `fwd-camera` at `27c8f6e`. The branch was originally cut from the unrelated `main` root and was reset to `fwd-camera` first.

## What was done

- **Two new files, plus this handover:**
  - `docs/thesis/glossary.md`: every code, tag, label and abbreviation found, grouped by category and sorted alphabetically (natural order) within each. Each meaning is a verbatim quote with `file:line`, or `pdf p.N` for the PDFs.
  - `docs/thesis/glossary_flags.md`:
    - (a) UNDEFINED terms, with the places they are used;
    - (b) CONFLICTS: every meaning of each clashing code, with its source;
    - (c) PROPOSALS for disambiguating prefixes in the thesis text, **not applied**.
- **Nothing else changed.** No file, trial, result, world, parameter or branch was renamed, moved or edited.

## Method

1. **Mechanical inventory.** A regex sweep of all in-scope docs and the PDF text (`pdftotext -layout`, split by page) found about 420 code-shaped tokens. This list was the denominator.
2. **Seven parallel read-only extraction agents** each produced JSONL records (term, family, kind, verbatim quote, file, line). They were split as follows:
   - A/B/C/E/I/X codes
   - D/OI/F/T codes
   - G/H/L/M/N/O/P/Q codes and the H stages
   - R/S/U/V codes, phases and experiments
   - trial and dataset naming on disk, including the archives in `~/thesis_data`, batches and `eval/results`
   - worlds, models, `.parm` files, scripts, branches and dataflash messages, citing the ArduPilot and `~/ardupilot_ukf` source comments
   - estimator and build labels, metrics, and status labels and rulings
3. **Every record was checked mechanically.** The whitespace-normalised quote, or each " ... " fragment, must appear at the cited line or a few lines below it.
   - Result: 2168 records kept, all verified.
   - 22 records are used-undefined directory or file names. For these, only the existence of the path was checked.
4. **Grouping and conflicts.**
   - Records were grouped by term, and each term was placed in one category: the most frequent among its records.
   - A **conflict** is a term with two or more distinct meaning families among its defined or superseded records.
   - Cross-agent duplicates of the same meaning were merged by an explicit list in the assembler:
     - `T_A`…`T_D`, `results_pre_*`, `rgs_check_tex`, `phase5b`, `d15_port`, `ukf-jitter-fix`, `board-h743`
     - `set`, `V-L`, `_half`, `_fix`, `_v4`, `_robust`, `SUPERSEDED`, `S2v2`, `S3v2`, `_far`
5. **The scratch files are not committed:** the scripts, raw JSONL and verification output. They live in the session scratchpad.
