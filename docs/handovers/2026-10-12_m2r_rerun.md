### 1.1 Why the old M2-R did not complete, and the choice of stimulus (from the stored logs; no tuning run was made)

- **Plain "stop the flow" is not dependable.** The wbench4 flow-kill attempts (`flow_bridge.py` SIGKILLed below 0.75 m,
  restarted at touchdown) peaked at op-link |v| 0.015, 0.267, 0.25 and 0.29 m/s, none ≥ 0.2 for 1 s. The natural
  landings gave 0.10–0.69 m/s; the one runaway ended 1.9 s after the disarm. B8a (flow lost for the whole descent;
  `wbench4_hoverB_173841` BIN 47) gave NO horizontal runaway: after 5 s without flow EKF3 stopped aiding
  (const-pos), and when flow returned it restarted relative aiding with the velocity reset, so the error vanished.
- **What produced the persistent runaways was CORRUPT flow that EKF3 ACCEPTED near the floor**, not a dropout:
  - `wbench4_hoverB_173841` BIN 49 (the 42.6 m/s case after B9a; a LAND from 1.5 m with the real flow running):
    XKF5 NI 121 at HAGL 0.83 (FIX −0.17 rad/s), FIY +0.57 rad/s at HAGL 0.35 with OF quality 146 and flowY 0.68 rad/s;
    VN 0.58 m/s at touchdown with NI 255, then VN/VE ramping (0.25 and 0.4 m/s² rising) while the vehicle sat still,
    NI decaying only to 136 by +25 s; the gate never re-opened in the 90 s watched.
  - `wbench2_hoverB_112751` BIN 26, F 86–100: FIX +0.40/+0.24 rad/s at HAGL 0.68/0.42 (OF quality 163, 46), NI 255 from
    touchdown, VN −0.47 → −3.1 m/s over 13 s on the ground.
  - F8 itself (`2026-10-12_f8_investigation.md` §2.1): the few frames that passed near the floor carried wild flow
    (800–3000 px/s), and the error EKF3 carried into the touchdown became the phantom velocity.
  - Mechanism (F8 §2.3–2.6): an error accepted while the flow was being fused keeps P_v small, so on the ground the
    flow gate (≈ 3σ of P_v) locks that axis out; the other axis stays accepted, so the zero-velocity fusion and the
    AID_NONE fall-back stay off; a tilt error then ramps the locked axis. A dropout, by contrast, lets P_v grow (and
    after 5 s resets the aiding), which is why the flow-kill recipe did not persist.
  - The B4c tilt-drop (192 m/s, vz first, a disarmed 65° drop) is a different mechanism and is NOT used.
- **The stimulus registered here is a controlled version of that:** a velocity-equivalent bias on ONE flow axis during
  the final descent, cut back to the real flow above the floor, landing in ALT_HOLD.

### 1.2 Stimulus (session `m2r`: `tools/run_wbench.sh m2r --prefix wbench5 --checks all`)

- **The bias bridge.** In this session `flow_bridge_real` is `realflight/sitl_bench/flow_bias_bridge.py`: it
  subclasses `flow_bridge.FlowBridge` and, in "pass" mode (its start state), sends exactly what `flow_bridge.py`
  sends. In "bias" mode it adds b = v_b / r to `flow_rate_y` only (`flow_rate_x` untouched), with
  v_b = v·min(1, t/1.0 s) and r = LiDAR range − 0.13 m (the camera height), i.e. EKF3 is shown a body velocity v_b
  too large on one axis whatever the height. When |b| would exceed 2.0 rad/s (under FLOW_MAX 2.5), or the range is
  stale, it CUTS back to "pass" (the real flow, no artificial dropout); the cut height is LiDAR range 0.13 + v/2 m
  (0.63 m at v = 1.0, 0.88 m at v = 1.5). Every OPTICAL_FLOW it sends is logged (`flow_bias.csv`: mode, range, raw,
  bias, sent). Offline check: `realflight/sitl_bench/tests/test_flow_bias.py` (3 tests).
- **One attempt** (`bench.m2r_attempt`):
  1. M1 if a landing happened in this boot (so every attempt starts from a fresh boot); the watcher confirms (M2's 5 s
     still and slow included); the bias in "pass".
  2. The flight script (H10) arms and takes off to 1.5 m in GUIDED, holds 4 s (`STEP 2 hold start` + 1 s).
  3. The simulated pilot: throttle centred (1500), the mode switch to ALT_HOLD; 1.0 s later the bias on (v, ramp
     1.0 s); 2.0 s later throttle 1300 (ALT_HOLD descent ≈ 0.6 m/s). The bias cuts itself at the cut height.
  4. Touchdown = true base-link height < 0.23 m (cap 20 s). The harness commands "pass" at touchdown (normally already
     cut), throttle to 1000, and **the pilot disarms 1.0 s after touchdown** (M3: within 2 s; a forced MAVLink disarm
     standing in for the stick). Once the disarm is seen, the hover flight script is stopped (SIGTERM; its hold loop
     does not exit on a disarm), then the switch goes back to GUIDED.
  5. The probes (§1.3) from the disarm until the operator-link |v| < 0.2 m/s for 1.0 s continuously, or 40 s.
  6. Observation: up to 8 s for the watcher to re-confirm in the same boot.
- **The fixed schedule (no adaptation): v = 1.0, 1.0, 1.5, 1.5 m/s**, four attempts, all run whatever the outcome.
- **Then M1 and the flight-script arm** (T4): reboot, 45 s settle, the origin re-established and verified, the
  watcher restarted with its previous arguments, then the flight script with profile `[]` (arm, then LAND on the
  ground).
- **No ALT_HOLD in M2 terms:** the watcher's M2 is informational while armed, so nothing in steps 2–4 can withdraw
  for "vel"; the H10 guard keeps the flight script from commanding LAND in ALT_HOLD.

### 1.3 Probes during the runaway

- **File gate:** the watcher's `watch_heartbeat` (what `fly_trial.watch_ok` reads) polled at 20 Hz in a thread, with
  the op-link |v|, from the disarm to the end of the probe phase (`<attempt>_polls.json`).
- **Flight-script gate:** a flight script with profile `[]` launched 0.5 s after the operator link first sees
  |v| ≥ 0.2 after the disarm; it waits in `require_watcher_before_arming` (60 s); it is stopped (SIGTERM) when the
  runaway ends (|v| < 0.2 for 1.0 s) or at the cap.
- **FC-side MAVLink probes:** an operator non-force arm in GUIDED (system ID 255). A probe is made only when ALL hold,
  re-checked immediately before EVERY probe: now ≥ the latest watcher HEARTBEAT forwarded to the FC
  (`relay_gcs_hb.csv`, new in the relay: every watcher → FC HEARTBEAT, wall time) + 3.5 s (`FS_GCS_TIMEOUT` 3 s);
  the file gate shows `box=0`; the op-link |v| ≥ 0.2; ≥ 2.0 s since the previous probe. An arm that succeeds is
  disarmed at once and recorded. **No probe is made inside the ~3.3 s FS_GCS_TIMEOUT gap**: an arm there would be accepted by
  design (the open safety statement, closed procedurally by ruling 5's 10 s card rule); it is not re-tested.

### 1.4 Definitions

- **t_dis:** the watcher's own first `mocap_watch_vel.csv` sample with `fc_armed=False` after the attempt's arm.
- **t_on:** the first `mocap_watch_vel.csv` sample at or after t_dis with |v| ≥ 0.2 m/s and mocap speed < 0.05 m/s.
  An attempt "has a runaway" iff t_on exists within 5 s of t_dis.
- **t_end:** the first `mocap_watch_vel.csv` sample after t_on with |v| < 0.2 m/s (or the end of the probe phase if
  none: "capped").
- **Runaway duration** = t_end − t_on (watcher's view); the op-link view is reported alongside.

### 1.5 Criteria (each attempt judged separately; all reported AS WORDED)

**Validity of an attempt (void if any fails; a void attempt counts as NOT producing a runaway):**
- R-a: no BREACH and no crash disarm (FC text "Crash") from the arm to the next M1;
- R-b: the true horizontal excursion from the ALT_HOLD entry point ≤ 2.0 m before touchdown;
- R-c: no "EKF Failsafe" FC text before touchdown (R2);
- R-d: `flow_bias.csv` shows "pass" mode for every message sent after the touchdown command (the runaway is EKF3's own,
  not the bias);
- R-e: the truth speed (forwarder, 0.5 s baseline) < 0.05 m/s at every `mocap_watch_vel.csv` sample in [t_on, t_end]
  (the watcher's own mocap speed column).

**T1 (timing; independent of the runaway's duration; every valid attempt with a runaway):**
- (i) watcher side: the last `mocap_watch_heartbeat.csv` row at or before t_on + 0.5 s shows `confirmed` = 0
  (rows are written every 1 s tick and at once on every change), and the `STOP_LOG.txt` "confirmation 0" line in
  force at t_on + 0.5 s contains a "vel …" reason (other reasons, e.g. yaw, are recorded, not failing);
- (ii) FC side: no watcher HEARTBEAT forwarded to the FC (`relay_gcs_hb.csv`) in (t_on + 0.5 s, t_end];
- (iii) every `mocap_watch_heartbeat.csv` row in [t_on + 0.5 s, t_end] has `confirmed` = 0.

**T2 (no arm while |v| ≥ 0.2; every valid attempt with a runaway):**
- (a) file gate: every 20 Hz poll in [t_on + 0.5 s, t_end] shows `box=0` (so `watch_ok` refuses);
- (b) flight-script gate: the flight script launched during the runaway does not log "Armed." before it is stopped;
- (c) FC side (evaluated on each attempt where at least one FC-side probe was made while the op-link |v| ≥ 0.2; else
  NOT EVALUATED for that attempt): every probe is refused: no `COMMAND_ACK` result 0 and no ARMED heartbeat within
  2 s, and the FC texts of the probe include "GCS failsafe" (the FC's own refusal of a vehicle whose watcher has
  withdrawn);
- (d) validity of (c), post hoc (V1b): dataflash `XKF1` core 0 √(VN² + VE² + VD²) ≥ 0.2 m/s within ± 0.5 s of each
  counted probe (mapped through the session's `op_systime.csv`); a probe failing V1b is not counted.

**T3 (no LAND from M2):** 0 watcher "LAND command sent" and no BREACH from the disarm to the next M1.

**T4 (after M1, arming allowed; once, at the end):** (e) a `GPS_GLOBAL_ORIGIN` with the sent lat/lon received after
the reboot; (f) the watcher confirms within 90 s of its restart, with the op-link |v| < 0.2 m/s over the 5 s before
the confirmation; (g) the flight script logs "Safety watcher confirmed before arming" and "Armed.".

**M2-R result:**
- **PASS** iff T1, T2 (a)(b), T3 hold on every valid attempt with a runaway; T2(c) holds on every attempt where it is
  evaluated and is evaluated (with V1b) on ≥ 1 attempt; and T4 holds.
- **FAIL** iff any of these fails on an evaluated attempt.
- **NOT COMPLETED** iff no valid attempt has a runaway, or T2(c) is evaluated on no attempt.
- **Dependability of the stimulus (reported separately):** the recipe is DEPENDABLE at a given v iff BOTH attempts at
  that v are valid and have a runaway lasting ≥ 5.0 s (watcher's view, t_end − t_on). k of 4 is reported. If neither v
  is dependable, that is reported as such (the criterion is not adjusted).

**Observations (not pass items):** the runaway size, onset, duration (watcher, op link, XKF1 cores 0/1), whether it was
capped (never re-captured); the bias actually absorbed by EKF3 before the cut (XKF1 VN/VE against truth); the true
touchdown speed; the re-confirmation in the same boot after the runaway (expected ≥ 5 s after t_end); the lag
between the watcher's and the op link's view of the disarm (X1/B13 context).

### 1.6 Why the length suffices (STANDING RULE 2026-10-10)

- Each attempt is ~25 s armed (take-off ~3 s, hold 4 s, ALT_HOLD ~3 s, descent ~2–3 s) plus ≤ 40 s of ground probes;
  the stimulus is the touchdown, and 40 s covers F8's 6–10 s lock-outs and shows a non-re-capturing one.
- Four attempts give two at each v, the minimum for the dependability statement; each M1 adds ~60 s (reboot, settle).
- Whole session ≈ 15 min wall-clock; one simulation, under FLIGHT_LOCK; nothing heavy in parallel.
