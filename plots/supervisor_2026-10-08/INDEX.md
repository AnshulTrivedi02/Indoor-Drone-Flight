## fig14_L_lap_by_lap.png: L: tag re-identification bounds the drift lap by lap

- **Status:** CONFIRMATORY (pre-registered H-L1, P-L4)
- **Caption:** Keyframe FGO with tags at UNKNOWN positions, T_A_r1–r3 (3 lines per style), RMSE per 6 m lap in the first tag's frame. With loop closure (tags re-identified by id) the live error falls after lap 1 and stays 0.02–0.04 m (H-L1 PASS: lap 5 ≤ 1.5 × lap 1); without it (a new landmark per pass) it grows 4.5–5.5× over five laps (P-L4 PASS), and hindsight does not reliably help. An FGO capability, not an FGO-vs-filter result: the filters have no loop-closure equivalent without a different design. Log scale.
- **Data source:** eval/results/expL/T_A_r*_t245.json (per_lap_rmse_m)
- **Commit:** `c506533`

## fig15_L_compute_cost.png: L: per-update solve time over the flight (diagnostic)

- **Status:** DIAGNOSTIC (not a pre-registered metric)
- **Caption:** Mean iSAM2 update time per 1000-keyframe block, keyframe FGO with retained history (30 Hz keyframes, the thesis cadence), 3 flights per mode. With loop closure the cost stays at tens of ms; without it each pass adds a landmark and the cost climbs to 0.1–1.4 s per update in 1000-keyframe means (per-update p99 1.3–2.2 s). Workstation Python with numerical-Jacobian factors, six replays in parallel: diagnostic only. A real drone would need a much lower keyframe rate, bounded landmarks and compiled factors.
- **Data source:** lc_fgo_replay progress logs (per 1000 keyframes), lc_fgo_*_t245/stats.json
